Category 4 may later become a cross-cutting evidence layer spanning Team, Business, Product, and Documentation. Assessment-Framework Design is unnumbered because its questions are team decisions and will not form part of the final startup checklist.

## Provisional placements

- **Q117** is under Team Strength because it tests the alignment of operating authority and founder power. It could alternatively sit under formal governance.
- **Q139** is under evidence availability because these records are unlikely to be accessible during automated pre-screening. Substantively, it also concerns integrity.
- Sparsely populated subcategories do not necessarily indicate coverage gaps yet. Some existing questions combine several ideas and may later need decomposition.
