# 1 Submission provenance and usability

- Founder submission [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Was the funding application submitted directly by a founder rather than an advisor, consultant, intermediary, or fundraising agent? Keywords: applicant contact founder role submitted by application contact. Hint: Pass only with explicit founder status. Fail explicit non-founder submission and route to Rejected for other reason. Otherwise Unclear and request the applicant's role.
- Application identity: Are the startup name, legal name, applicant name, founder role, email, country, city, website, and LinkedIn URL supplied and mutually coherent? Keywords: name legal_name contact country address_city website linkedin. Hint: Treat missing or conflicting identity fields as Unclear and request corrections.
- Submitted Dealum record: Is there a populated Dealum application record with substantive answers rather than a draft-like, empty, placeholder, or test submission? Keywords: Dealum Application answers application status placeholder test lorem ipsum. Hint: Fail only when the record explicitly establishes an empty or unusable submission; otherwise use Unclear for missing evidence.
- Pitch deck attachment: Is a pitch deck attached through Dealum and retrievable as a readable document? Keywords: pitch_deck pitch deck presentation attachment. Hint: A bare URL with no retrievable submitted file is Unclear. An explicitly empty or corrupt attachment is Fail. Request a readable deck.
- Financial plan attachment: Is the requested financial plan attached through Dealum and retrievable in a readable form? Keywords: sictic_financials sictic_confirm_financial_plan financial plan forecast budget spreadsheet attachment. Hint: A confirmation without a retrievable file is Unclear. Request the missing or readable file.
- Core answer coverage: Do the Dealum answers substantively cover problem, solution, business model, market, customers or users, team, technology, milestones, and funding request? Keywords: problem_summary solution_summary business_summary market_summary customers_and_users founders_commitments technologies road_map_next_milestone capital_seeking. Hint: Do not judge persuasiveness. Identify fields that are empty, placeholders, or too indeterminate to screen.

# 2 Swiss or Liechtenstein core

- Core-team location [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the core team, including key C-level roles, based in Switzerland or Liechtenstein? Keywords: CEO CTO COO founder location residence work location Switzerland Swiss Liechtenstein. Hint: Fail only when the submission confirms the entire core team is outside Switzerland and Liechtenstein; route to Rejected due to foreign country. Missing individual locations are Unclear.
- Core-team main activity [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Does the evidence show that the core team works on the startup as its main activity? Keywords: founders_commitments full-time main activity FTE employment dedication side job. Hint: Fail explicit absence of a qualifying full-time core team and route to Rejected for other reason. Otherwise request commitment percentages and other roles.
- Swiss or Liechtenstein AG [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the startup incorporated, or does it explicitly commit to incorporate, as an AG in Switzerland or Liechtenstein? Keywords: company_registered registered legal_name legal form AG stock corporation incorporation country Switzerland Liechtenstein. Hint: A foreign entity without an explicit qualifying reincorporation commitment fails and routes to Rejected due to foreign country. A merely vague intention is Unclear.
- Swiss equity issuer [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Will the proposed investment be made against equity, or an equity-converting instrument, in a company registered in Switzerland? Keywords: equity raise_instrument SAFE CLA convertible equity sharing Swiss company registered. Hint: Fail an explicitly non-equity financing or an issuer that will remain foreign; route foreign cases to Rejected due to foreign country and other cases to Rejected for other reason.

# 3 Team eligibility and completeness

- Non-solo operation [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the startup more than a one-person operation? Keywords: team_size founder cofounder employee active team member. Hint: Fail an explicitly solo-operated startup and route to Rejected for other reason. A claimed team size without identifiable people is Unclear.
- Qualifying second team member [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Besides the submitting founder, is at least one other full-time, active team member identified with a key operating role and equity participation? Keywords: cofounder key role full-time active equity shareholder advisor consultant. Hint: Advisors and consultants do not qualify. Fail only when the submission explicitly confirms no qualifying second member; otherwise request name, role, FTE, location, and equity status.
- Founder and team roster: Are all founders and key team members named consistently across the Dealum form and pitch deck? Keywords: founders team management leadership pitch deck founders_commitments team_size. Hint: Missing names, founders mentioned but not listed, or contradictory rosters are Unclear and require a corrected roster.
- Roles, location, commitment, and equity: For each founder and key team member, are operating role, work location, commitment level, and equity participation clear? Keywords: CEO CTO COO role location FTE commitment full-time equity shares options. Hint: Do not infer these attributes from titles or team size.

# 4 Venture eligibility

- Innovative scalable tech component [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Does the submission explicitly identify innovative technology, science, or IP that enables scalable business growth? Keywords: technologies innovation science intellectual property IP scalability business_model_scalability. Hint: Fail only when the submission explicitly describes a non-tech, non-science, non-IP business with no qualifying component; route to Rejected for other reason. Generic claims without explanation are Unclear.
- Incorporation age [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the startup no more than five years old measured from its incorporation date? Keywords: registered incorporation date founded company age company_registered. Hint: Calculate only from an explicit incorporation date. More than five years fails and routes to Rejected for other reason, even if operations started later; flag conflicting dates as Unclear.
- CHF 5M revenue potential [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Does the submission contain enough explicit, internally coherent information to support a reasonable chance of at least CHF 5M yearly revenue within five years after investment? Keywords: financial plan revenue forecast yearly revenue five years market size pricing customers scalability. Hint: This is an eligibility plausibility check, not investment-quality scoring. Unsupported assertions or missing forecasts are Unclear; fail only if the submitted forecast explicitly remains below the threshold.

# 5 Funding-round eligibility and completeness

- Valuation cap [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the startup's pre-money valuation no higher than CHF 8M? Keywords: valuation pre-money post-money CHF company valuation cap raise terms. Hint: Pass only with an explicit pre-money CHF valuation at or below CHF 8M. Above CHF 8M fails and routes to Rejected for other reason. A post-money value, valuation range, cap without clear basis, or foreign currency without submitted CHF equivalent is Unclear.
- Round-size cap [ELIGIBILITY GATE]: Is the total current round size no higher than CHF 2M? Keywords: capital_seeking capital_raised_current_round current round target total round size CHF. Hint: Assess total round size, not only the amount still open. Above CHF 2M fails and routes to Rejected for other reason. Ambiguous amount or currency is Unclear.
- Funding ask reconciliation: Are total round size, amount already committed or raised, and amount still sought stated separately and mathematically coherent? Keywords: capital_seeking capital_raised_current_round committed raised open remaining round. Hint: Show any arithmetic conflict; do not invent a definition for ambiguous fields.
- Instrument and equity path: Is the proposed funding instrument identified and is its path to equity in the Swiss issuer clear? Keywords: raise_instrument equity shares SAFE CLA convertible note loan grant token. Hint: Missing terms are Unclear. Apply the Swiss equity issuer gate separately from general financing completeness.
- Round timing: Is the intended closing date or fundraising timetable supplied and still current at the application or review date? Keywords: round_close_date_intentions application date close date timeline fundraising active round. Hint: An expired or contradictory date is Unclear unless the submission explicitly says the round is closed or inactive.
- Financing history: Are previous equity funding, non-dilutive funding, investors or funding sources, and total investments received clearly disclosed and mutually consistent? Keywords: investments_received investments_received_has equity_funding_sources non_dilutive_funding_received financing history. Hint: Do not require prior funding; require a clear zero or a coherent disclosure.

# 6 Business-information completeness

- Problem and solution: Are the problem, target user or customer, and proposed solution stated clearly enough to identify what is being solved and for whom? Keywords: problem_summary solution_summary customers_and_users. Hint: Assess screenability and completeness, not attractiveness.
- Product, technology, and IP: Are the product or service, enabling technologies, development status, and claimed IP or protection approach described? Keywords: solution technologies ip_protection_type product prototype patent software know-how. Hint: A legitimate statement that no formal IP protection exists may still be complete.
- Business model and scalability: Are the revenue model, principal cost drivers, and scalability mechanism described? Keywords: business_summary business_types business_cost_structure business_model_scalability pricing revenue model. Hint: Do not score the model; identify information gaps.
- Market and differentiation: Are target market, market-size basis, competitive alternatives, and unique value proposition covered? Keywords: market_summary market_estimation market_unique_value competition alternatives TAM SAM SOM. Hint: A market-size number without method or units is Unclear.
- Customers, traction, and revenue: Are customers or users, relevant usage or commercial traction, current revenue or MRR, and notable accomplishments disclosed with dates or periods where applicable? Keywords: customers_and_users revenue_mrr accomplishment_summary traction pilots LOI revenue users. Hint: Pre-revenue is acceptable if explicitly stated; do not equate zero traction with incompleteness.
- Roadmap and milestones: Are the next material product, regulatory, commercial, hiring, or financing milestones and expected timing supplied? Keywords: road_map_next_milestone roadmap milestone timeline regulatory approval launch. Hint: Request dates or sequencing when a milestone is too vague to screen.
- Classification fields: Are company stage, company category, industry, business type, and technology classifications present and consistent with the narrative and deck? Keywords: company_stage company_category industries business_types technologies. Hint: Treat taxonomy mismatch as Unclear, not as an eligibility failure.
- Prior SICTIC application: If the startup applied previously, does it disclose the prior application and material changes since then? Keywords: sictic_already_applied sictic_changes_since_last_application reapplied changes improvements. Hint: If it explicitly never applied, Pass. If prior application status is missing, use Unclear only when other evidence suggests a prior application.
- Event preference: Is any specific SICTIC event preference supplied, or is it clear that no specific event was requested? Keywords: sictic_pitch_event event vertical SID preference. Hint: This is an Ops-routing field, not an eligibility gate.

# 7 Cross-document consistency and freshness

- Legal and location consistency: Are legal name, registration status, legal form, incorporation country, address, and team locations consistent across the application and attachments? Keywords: legal_name registered company_registered country address pitch deck footer imprint. Hint: Cite every conflicting source and request one corrected statement with supporting evidence.
- Team consistency: Are founder names, roles, team size, locations, commitment, and equity claims consistent across all submitted materials? Keywords: founders_commitments team_size team slide founder role FTE equity. Hint: Never resolve discrepancies by choosing the more favorable source.
- Funding consistency: Are valuation, round size, amount raised, amount sought, instrument, and closing date consistent across the Dealum form, deck, and financial plan? Keywords: valuation capital_seeking capital_raised_current_round raise_instrument close date pitch deck financial plan. Hint: Identify whether values may refer to different rounds; if not explicit, use Unclear.
- Commercial and financial consistency: Are customer, user, revenue, MRR, forecast, burn, and milestone figures consistent across the application, deck, and financial plan? Keywords: revenue_mrr customers users forecast financial plan pitch deck accomplishments. Hint: Account for clearly labeled reporting periods; do not treat different dates as contradictions without checking them.
- Information freshness: Are time-sensitive figures and claims dated or attributable to a reporting period recent enough to understand their status at review time? Keywords: as of date current latest updated application date MRR customers valuation round. Hint: Do not impose a fixed maximum age. Mark Unclear when undated data could materially change eligibility or screening.
