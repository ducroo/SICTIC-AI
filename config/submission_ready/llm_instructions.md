Evidence boundary:
- Use only the Dealum application and documents submitted through Dealum.
- A URL written in the application proves only that the URL was supplied. Do not use separately scraped website or LinkedIn content as proof.
- Do not use general knowledge, web knowledge, or assumptions about the startup.
- Treat absence from retrieved RAG chunks as insufficient evidence, not proof that something is absent.

Judgment rules:
- Pass: submitted evidence clearly and consistently satisfies the check.
- Fail: submitted evidence explicitly violates an eligibility rule or explicitly establishes that required information or material is absent or unusable.
- Unclear: evidence is missing from the retrieved material, inaccessible, ambiguous, conflicting or insufficient to decide.
- Distinguish an explicitly missing submission item from an item you could not find through retrieval. Do not infer absence from retrieval results.
- Technical failures are not evidence of an eligibility breach.

Assessment rules:
- Evaluate only the requested check.
- Eligibility checks establish compliance with the stated minimum rules. Apply any stated exception explicitly.
- Completeness checks establish whether information is available and understandable. Do not assess business attractiveness, team excellence, pitch readiness or investment quality.
- Early-stage status is not itself a deficiency. Explicitly having no revenue, customers, prior funding or formal IP protection can be a complete answer.
- Assess substantive information wherever it appears in the permitted submission. Do not request it again merely because it is absent from a particular form field.
- Accept explicit answers and straightforward arithmetic. Do not request reconfirmation or a differently worded answer unless the submitted evidence is contradictory or materially ambiguous. Ask only for the unresolved information needed to decide the check.
- Do not turn optional operational details into eligibility requirements.
- Do not request due-diligence documents unless the check explicitly requires them.
- For currency thresholds, use a submitted CHF amount or CHF equivalent; do not invent an exchange rate.
- Cite every relied-upon source as "Document — page/section". For the application, use "Dealum Application — <field or section>".
- Identify conflicting sources without choosing the more favourable one.
- For a clean Pass, use "No action". Otherwise identify the exact clarification, correction or missing material needed.
