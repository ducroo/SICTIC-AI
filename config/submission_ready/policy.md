SICTIC initial screening policy, clarified for this workflow on 2026-10-31.

Public eligibility reference:
https://www.sictic.ch/startups/#investment-criteria

Submission-process reference:
https://www.sictic.ch/pitch/

The rules below incorporate the agreed screening clarifications on Swiss
presence, team composition and valuation. Apply these rules where wording in
the public references differs. This is a completeness and minimum-eligibility
screen, not due diligence or an investment recommendation.

Apply these rules:
- At least one active founder is based in Switzerland. More Swiss-based founders are preferable but not required; a Swiss company address alone is insufficient evidence.
- The startup is incorporated, or explicitly commits to incorporate before investment, as a stock corporation (AG/SA) in Switzerland. An entity that will remain in Liechtenstein or any other country does not qualify.
- The proposed investment provides equity, or converts into equity, in that Swiss company.
- The startup has at least one full-time active founder and at least one additional full-time operating co-founder or employee with a key role and equity participation. Advisors, consultants and planned hires do not qualify.
- A solo founder may qualify through the exception for serial founders with a strong exit track record, established by submitted evidence. State when this exception is used; it does not waive other eligibility requirements.
- The startup has an innovative technology, science or IP component that enables scalable growth.
- The startup is no more than five years old, measured from incorporation. A startup explicitly not yet incorporated does not fail the age check.
- The submission provides an understandable, internally coherent basis for reaching at least CHF 5m annual revenue within five years after investment. Existing revenue and proven scalability are not required.
- The proposed pre-money valuation is no higher than CHF 8m.
- The total current round is no higher than CHF 2m, including amounts already raised or committed within that round.
- The application is submitted directly by a founder.

Workflow routing:
- Missing, ambiguous or conflicting evidence requires clarification; it is not confirmed ineligibility.
- Confirmed absence of a Swiss-based founder or a qualifying Swiss entity is a geography eligibility concern. Other confirmed breaches are other eligibility concerns.
- Only recommend progression when the checklist supports it. Missing optional routing details alone do not prevent progression.
- At Application, propose Move to Under review or Send concerns to startup.
- At Under review, propose Move to Jury or Send concerns to startup.
- For an explicit assessment at any other or unknown stage, use Assessment only: report the findings without recommending a stage transition or outreach.
- These are proposed actions for human review, not automatic stage changes or a jury recommendation.
