SICTIC initial screening policy, last verified 2026-07-26.

Authoritative eligibility source:
https://www.sictic.ch/startups/#investment-criteria

Submission-process source:
https://www.sictic.ch/pitch/

Apply these rules:
- The core startup team, including key C-level roles, is based in Switzerland or Liechtenstein and works on the startup as its main activity.
- The startup is incorporated, or explicitly commits to incorporate, as a stock corporation (AG) in Switzerland or Liechtenstein.
- The proposed investment is against equity in a company registered in Switzerland.
- The startup has an innovative technology, science, or IP component that enables scalable growth.
- The startup is no more than five years old, measured from incorporation.
- The startup has a reasonable chance to reach at least CHF 5M in yearly revenue within five years after the investment.
- The pre-money valuation is no higher than CHF 8M.
- The current round size is no higher than CHF 2M.
- The application is submitted directly by a founder.
- The startup is not solo-operated: besides the founder, at least one other full-time, active team member has a key role and equity participation. Advisors and consultants do not qualify.

Workflow routing:
- Missing, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence: Under Review; request clarification.
- Confirmed absence of a Swiss or Liechtenstein core: Rejected due to foreign country.
- Any other confirmed eligibility breach: Rejected for other reason.
- A complete submission with no confirmed eligibility breach: eligible to move to Jury. This is not a jury recommendation.
