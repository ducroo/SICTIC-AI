# 4 Financials-General

## Historical & Current Financials

### Annual Financial Statements

Are the formal balance sheets and income statements for the last three years available and complete with accruals and deferrals?

Ensure the P\&L matches the tax filings and that year-end adjustments are clearly visible.

**Keywords:** Balance Sheet Bilanz P\&L Income Statement Erfolgsrechnung Annual Report Jahresrechnung Accruals Abgrenzungen Deferrals

### Interim Management Accounts

Are current Year-to-Date financials provided on a monthly or quarterly basis and compared against the annual budget?

Look for significant variances between the budget and actuals that might indicate unbudgeted spending or missed revenue targets.

**Keywords:** YTD Interim Report Management Accounts Budget vs Actual Variance Analysis Monthly Financials Quarterly Update

### Cash Position & Liquidity

Can the current cash-on-hand be verified via bank statements dated within the last 30 days for all accounts?

Cross-check the total balance on the statements with the "Cash" line item in the most recent interim management accounts.

**Keywords:** Bank Statement Kontoauszug Cash Balance Liquidity Bank Account Reconciliation Cash-on-hand Proof of Funds

### Burn Rate & Runway Analysis

Is there a cash flow statement that clearly identifies the historical monthly net burn and the remaining runway?

Calculate the average monthly burn over the last 6 months to see if the company's "runway" claim is based on realistic spending trends.

**Keywords:** Cash Flow Statement Burn Rate Runway Cash Burn Net Burn Operating Cash Flow Liquidity Runway Financial Runway

## Projections & Performance Metrics

### Financial Forecasting Model

Does the company have a 3-5 year financial model that integrates the P\&L balance sheet and cash flow with transparent assumptions?

Evaluate the "Growth Assumptions" for realism against historical performance; flag any aggressive growth curves without clear justification.

**Keywords:** Financial Model Projections Forecasting Integrated Model Assumptions Business Plan Growth Drivers Cost Drivers

### Operational KPI Reporting

Are periodic management reports tracking core business model KPIs like CAC LTV churn or gross margins available?

Verify that the formulas used to calculate KPIs—especially LTV and CAC—are standard and consistently applied over time.

**Keywords:** KPI Dashboard Unit Economics CAC Customer Acquisition Cost LTV Lifetime Value Churn Rate ARR MRR Gross Margin

### Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Plan

Is there a clear summary of planned investments in infrastructure or equipment required to achieve the financial projections?

Match the timing of the CapEx plan with the "Cash Out" lines in the financial model to ensure the funding for these investments is secured.

**Keywords:** CapEx Capital Expenditure Investment Plan Asset Purchase Infrastructure Spend Equipment Lease Production Plant

## Liabilities & Contractual Obligations

### Debt & Credit Instruments

Are all signed loan agreements promissory notes and credit lines with banks or private individuals disclosed?

Check for covenants or "change of control" clauses that could be triggered by an investment round.

**Keywords:** Loan Agreement Darlehensvertrag Credit Line Promissory Note Schuldschein Debt Instrument Interest Rate Repayment Schedule

### Fixed Lease Obligations

Are copies of all active lease and rental agreements for offices production sites and hardware provided?

Summarize the total annual fixed lease commitment to understand the "unavoidable" portion of the monthly burn rate.

**Keywords:** Lease Agreement Rental Agreement Mietvertrag Office Lease Equipment Leasing Fleet Management Machinery Lease

### Fundraising & Success Fees

Are all contracts regarding finder’s fees or fundraiser commissions for past or current rounds disclosed?

Identify any high commission percentages that might deviate from market standards or impact the net proceeds of the current round.

**Keywords:** Finder's Fee Success Fee Brokerage Agreement Commission Fundraising Mandate Engagement Letter Placement Fee

### Contingent Liabilities

Is there a summary of any off-balance-sheet liabilities such as financial guarantees or indemnities?

Focus on potential legal claims or environmental liabilities that are not yet recorded on the formal balance sheet.

**Keywords:** Contingent Liability Off-balance-sheet Guarantee Bürgschaft Indemnity Warranty Liability Provision

## Strategic Positioning & Exit

### Exit Scenario Analysis

Has a strategic overview of potential liquidity events including target acquirers and M\&A benchmarks been provided?

Check if the "comparable transactions" listed are recent and truly relevant to the company’s specific industry and stage.

**Keywords:** Exit Strategy M\&A Targets Liquidity Event IPO Readiness Acquisition Benchmarks Valuation Multiples Transaction History

### Dividend & Distribution History

Are there records of all dividends share buybacks or distributions made to shareholders since the company's inception?

Compare payout dates with periods of low liquidity to assess if distributions impacted the company's operational stability.

**Keywords:** Dividend Distribution Share Buyback Capital Repayment Shareholder Payout Profit Distribution Dividendenbeschluss
