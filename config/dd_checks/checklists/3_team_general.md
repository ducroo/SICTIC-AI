# 3 Team-General

## Organizational Structure & Transparency

### Team Overview

Does the documentation include a current organizational chart and a staff list detailing work locations and employment percentages (FTE)?

Reconcile the total headcount on the list with the visual chart and flag any missing FTE values or locations.

**Keywords:** Org Chart Organigramm Employee List Staff Directory FTE Employment Percentage Work Location Headcount

### Role Definitions

Are formal job descriptions available for all key personnel that explicitly define responsibilities and reporting lines?

Ensure every C-suite and lead role has a specific JD that clarifies their position within the hierarchy.

**Keywords:** Job Description JD Role Profile Responsibilities Reporting Line Accountability Organizational Role

### Hiring Standards

Is there evidence of a standardized hiring process including offer templates and a DSG/GDPR-compliant candidate privacy notice?

Verify the presence of a privacy notice that explicitly covers candidate data handling under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG).

**Keywords:** Hiring Process Recruitment Policy Offer Letter Template GDPR DSG Privacy Notice Candidate Data Onboarding

## Founder-Specific Verification (KYC & Risk)

### Identification

Are valid passports or government ID cards provided for all founders to verify identity and right to work?

Check expiration dates and ensure names match legal incorporation and employment documents exactly.

**Keywords:** Passport ID Card Identity Verification KYC Founder ID Government ID Right to Work

### Financial & Legal Integrity

Are there recent debt collection (Betreibungsauszüge) and criminal record (Strafregisterauszüge) extracts for the founding team?

Documents must be dated within the last 6 months to be considered valid; flag any entries related to financial misconduct or active debt enforcement.

**Keywords:** Betreibungsauszug Debt Collection Register Strafregisterauszug Criminal Record Integrity Check Background Check Solvency

### Commitment Verification

Do the founders' contracts contain clauses requiring 100% dedication and are any deviations explicitly justified?

Flag any founder working less than 100% or holding external operational roles as a "Commitment Risk" unless board-approved.

**Keywords:** Full-time commitment 100% Dedication Exclusivity Clause Side Activities Secondary Employment Founder Contract

### Restrictive Covenants

Are there signed written Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation agreements for all founders that comply with Swiss legal standards?

Verify that these covenants are in "written form" as required by Swiss law for enforceability and check for reasonable geographic/temporal limits.

**Keywords:** Non-Compete Konkurrenzverbot Non-Solicitation Restrictive Covenant Written Form Swiss Law Post-termination

## Employment Contract Hygiene

### Contract Regularization

Is there documented evidence that all oral agreements have been converted into formal signed written employment contracts?

Any missing signatures or "draft" statuses for active employees should be flagged as a high-risk "Regularization Gap."

**Keywords:** Employment Contract Written Agreement Regularization Oral Contract Signed Agreement Contract Audit

### Mandatory Written Clauses

Are specific terms regarding notice periods overtime compensation and IP assignment explicitly stated in writing?

Check for explicit deviations from Swiss Code of Obligations (CO) defaults and ensure the company owns all employee-created intellectual property.

**Keywords:** Notice Period Kündigungsfrist Overtime Überstunden IP Assignment Intellectual Property Work Product Swiss CO

### Team Credentials

Are CVs educational diplomas and work certificates (Arbeitszeugnisse) available to validate the expertise of all permanent staff?

Ensure the documentation trail is complete for all technical and leadership roles to support the claimed level of expertise.

**Keywords:** CV Resume Diploma Education Certificate Arbeitszeugnis Work Report Reference Letter Qualifications

### Work Authorization

Do all non-Swiss resident employees based in Switzerland hold valid work permits (L B C or G)?

Verify that the permit type aligns with the employee’s nationality and that the expiration dates are current.

**Keywords:** Work Permit Arbeitsbewilligung L Permit B Permit C Permit G Permit Residency Status Work Authorization

## Equity & Performance

### ESOP/PSOP Administration

Are formal option plan documents and a grant ledger provided that track pool size and individual allocations?

Reconcile the "Total Pool" in the plan against the "Allocated Grants" in the ledger to ensure no over-allocation has occurred.

**Keywords:** ESOP PSOP Phantom Stock Option Plan Grant Ledger Vesting Cliff Exercise Price Option Pool

### Performance Governance

Is there evidence of a functional performance management framework such as periodic OKR reviews or peer feedback?

Search for documentation of repeated review cycles (e.g. quarterly notes) rather than ad-hoc isolated feedback.

**Keywords:** Performance Management OKR Objectives and Key Results Performance Review Peer Feedback KPI Appraisal Cycle
