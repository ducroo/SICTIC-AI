# 3 Team-General

## Organizational Structure & Transparency

* Team Overview: Does the documentation include a current organizational chart and a staff list detailing work locations and employment percentages (FTE)? keywords: Org Chart Organigramm Employee List Staff Directory FTE Employment Percentage Work Location Headcount hints: Reconcile the total headcount on the list with the visual chart and flag any missing FTE values or locations.  
* Role Definitions: Are formal job descriptions available for all key personnel that explicitly define responsibilities and reporting lines? keywords: Job Description JD Role Profile Responsibilities Reporting Line Accountability Organizational Role hints: Ensure every C-suite and lead role has a specific JD that clarifies their position within the hierarchy.  
* Hiring Standards: Is there evidence of a standardized hiring process including offer templates and a DSG/GDPR-compliant candidate privacy notice? keywords: Hiring Process Recruitment Policy Offer Letter Template GDPR DSG Privacy Notice Candidate Data Onboarding hints: Verify the presence of a privacy notice that explicitly covers candidate data handling under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG).

## Founder-Specific Verification (KYC & Risk)

* Identification: Are valid passports or government ID cards provided for all founders to verify identity and right to work? keywords: Passport ID Card Identity Verification KYC Founder ID Government ID Right to Work hints: Check expiration dates and ensure names match legal incorporation and employment documents exactly.  
* Financial & Legal Integrity: Are there recent debt collection (Betreibungsauszüge) and criminal record (Strafregisterauszüge) extracts for the founding team? keywords: Betreibungsauszug Debt Collection Register Strafregisterauszug Criminal Record Integrity Check Background Check Solvency hints: Documents must be dated within the last 6 months to be considered valid; flag any entries related to financial misconduct or active debt enforcement.  
* Commitment Verification: Do the founders' contracts contain clauses requiring 100% dedication and are any deviations explicitly justified? keywords: Full-time commitment 100% Dedication Exclusivity Clause Side Activities Secondary Employment Founder Contract hints: Flag any founder working less than 100% or holding external operational roles as a "Commitment Risk" unless board-approved.  
* Restrictive Covenants: Are there signed written Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation agreements for all founders that comply with Swiss legal standards? keywords: Non-Compete Konkurrenzverbot Non-Solicitation Restrictive Covenant Written Form Swiss Law Post-termination hints: Verify that these covenants are in "written form" as required by Swiss law for enforceability and check for reasonable geographic/temporal limits.

## Employment Contract Hygiene

* Contract Regularization: Is there documented evidence that all oral agreements have been converted into formal signed written employment contracts? keywords: Employment Contract Written Agreement Regularization Oral Contract Signed Agreement Contract Audit hints: Any missing signatures or "draft" statuses for active employees should be flagged as a high-risk "Regularization Gap."  
* Mandatory Written Clauses: Are specific terms regarding notice periods overtime compensation and IP assignment explicitly stated in writing? keywords: Notice Period Kündigungsfrist Overtime Überstunden IP Assignment Intellectual Property Work Product Swiss CO hints: Check for explicit deviations from Swiss Code of Obligations (CO) defaults and ensure the company owns all employee-created intellectual property.  
* Team Credentials: Are CVs educational diplomas and work certificates (Arbeitszeugnisse) available to validate the expertise of all permanent staff? keywords: CV Resume Diploma Education Certificate Arbeitszeugnis Work Report Reference Letter Qualifications hints: Ensure the documentation trail is complete for all technical and leadership roles to support the claimed level of expertise.  
* Work Authorization: Do all non-Swiss resident employees based in Switzerland hold valid work permits (L B C or G)? keywords: Work Permit Arbeitsbewilligung L Permit B Permit C Permit G Permit Residency Status Work Authorization hints: Verify that the permit type aligns with the employee’s nationality and that the expiration dates are current.

## Equity & Performance

* ESOP/PSOP Administration: Are formal option plan documents and a grant ledger provided that track pool size and individual allocations? keywords: ESOP PSOP Phantom Stock Option Plan Grant Ledger Vesting Cliff Exercise Price Option Pool hints: Reconcile the "Total Pool" in the plan against the "Allocated Grants" in the ledger to ensure no over-allocation has occurred.  
* Performance Governance: Is there evidence of a functional performance management framework such as periodic OKR reviews or peer feedback? keywords: Performance Management OKR Objectives and Key Results Performance Review Peer Feedback KPI Appraisal Cycle hints: Search for documentation of repeated review cycles (e.g. quarterly notes) rather than ad-hoc isolated feedback.
