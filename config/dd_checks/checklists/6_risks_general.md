# 6 Risks-General

## Strategic Risk & Governance

### Strategic Risk Register

Does the company maintain a comprehensive log of identified business technical and operational risks including probability impact and mitigation owners?

Check if the register is regularly updated and if mitigation owners are active employees with relevant authority.

**Keywords:** Risk Register Risk Log Risk Matrix Probability Impact Mitigation Strategy Operational Risk Business Risk Technical Risk

### Conflict of Interest Disclosures

Are there signed declarations from all founders and key employees regarding external interests or related-party transactions?

Cross-reference external board seats with the "Commitment Verification" from the Team section to ensure no undisclosed operational conflicts.

**Keywords:** Conflict of Interest Disclosure Related Party Transaction Board Seat Competing Interest External Activity Side Business Shareholder Agreement

### Risk Management Framework

Is there evidence of formal internal policies and controls for assessing and managing corporate risk?

Verify if the framework is actually integrated into board meetings or management reports rather than being a static document.

**Keywords:** Risk Management Framework Internal Control System ICS Risk Policy Governance Compliance Procedure Risk Assessment

### Material Adverse Threats

Has the company provided a summary of external factors like regulatory shifts or technological obsolescence that could impact success?

Look for specific mention of upcoming legislation or industry shifts that could invalidate current business models or revenue streams.

**Keywords:** Material Adverse Effect Regulatory Change Geopolitical Risk Technological Obsolescence Market Threat External Risk Factor Risk Summary

## Insurance & Liability

### Insurance Portfolio

Is there a complete inventory of active policies including D\&O Professional Indemnity Cyber and Key Person insurance?

Verify that the coverage limits are appropriate for the company's current stage and industry risk profile.

**Keywords:** Insurance Policy D\&O Liability Professional Indemnity Cyber Insurance Key Person Insurance General Liability Insurance Certificate Premium

### Uninsured Risk Exposure

Has the company disclosed all known non-insured risks along with the rationale and potential financial exposure?

Evaluate the potential "worst-case" financial impact of disclosed uninsured risks against the company's current cash runway.

**Keywords:** Uninsured Risk Self-insurance Disclosure Risk Exposure Liability Gaps Insurance Waiver Financial Risk

### Claims History

Is there a report of all past and pending insurance claims including resolution status and premium impacts?

Frequent claims in a specific category (e.g. Cyber or Professional Liability) may indicate systemic operational weaknesses.

**Keywords:** Claims History Pending Claims Insurance Claim Loss Run Report Claim Resolution Litigation Settlement

## Competitive & Market Risks

### Competitor Threat Analysis

Is there a matrix identifying key competitors and assessing their potential to disrupt the company's market share?

Check if the analysis includes "indirect competitors" or emerging startups that could pivot into the company's core space.

**Keywords:** Competitor Analysis Threat Matrix Market Disruption Vulnerability Assessment Competitive Risk Market Share Loss Rivalry Analysis

### Supply Chain & Dependency Risks

Are risks associated with single-source suppliers or critical third-party service providers documented?

Identify dependencies on specific cloud providers or hardware manufacturers where a service interruption would halt operations.

**Keywords:** Supply Chain Risk Single Source Supplier Critical Vendor Dependency Dependency Mapping Vendor Risk Procurement Risk Single Point of Failure

### Intellectual Property Risks

Is there an assessment of IP-related threats such as patent expirations or the emergence of patent trolls?

Review "Freedom to Operate" (FTO) reports to ensure the company isn't unknowingly infringing on existing third-party patents.

**Keywords:** IP Risk Patent Expiration Patent Troll Freedom to Operate FTO IP Litigation Infringement Risk IP Portfolio Assessment
