# 6 Risks-General

## Strategic Risk & Governance

* Strategic Risk Register: Does the company maintain a comprehensive log of identified business technical and operational risks including probability impact and mitigation owners? keywords: Risk Register Risk Log Risk Matrix Probability Impact Mitigation Strategy Operational Risk Business Risk Technical Risk hints: Check if the register is regularly updated and if mitigation owners are active employees with relevant authority.  
* Conflict of Interest Disclosures: Are there signed declarations from all founders and key employees regarding external interests or related-party transactions? keywords: Conflict of Interest Disclosure Related Party Transaction Board Seat Competing Interest External Activity Side Business Shareholder Agreement hints: Cross-reference external board seats with the "Commitment Verification" from the Team section to ensure no undisclosed operational conflicts.  
* Risk Management Framework: Is there evidence of formal internal policies and controls for assessing and managing corporate risk? keywords: Risk Management Framework Internal Control System ICS Risk Policy Governance Compliance Procedure Risk Assessment hints: Verify if the framework is actually integrated into board meetings or management reports rather than being a static document.  
* Material Adverse Threats: Has the company provided a summary of external factors like regulatory shifts or technological obsolescence that could impact success? keywords: Material Adverse Effect Regulatory Change Geopolitical Risk Technological Obsolescence Market Threat External Risk Factor Risk Summary hints: Look for specific mention of upcoming legislation or industry shifts that could invalidate current business models or revenue streams.

## Insurance & Liability

* Insurance Portfolio: Is there a complete inventory of active policies including D\&O Professional Indemnity Cyber and Key Person insurance? keywords: Insurance Policy D\&O Liability Professional Indemnity Cyber Insurance Key Person Insurance General Liability Insurance Certificate Premium hints: Verify that the coverage limits are appropriate for the company's current stage and industry risk profile.  
* Uninsured Risk Exposure: Has the company disclosed all known non-insured risks along with the rationale and potential financial exposure? keywords: Uninsured Risk Self-insurance Disclosure Risk Exposure Liability Gaps Insurance Waiver Financial Risk hints: Evaluate the potential "worst-case" financial impact of disclosed uninsured risks against the company's current cash runway.  
* Claims History: Is there a report of all past and pending insurance claims including resolution status and premium impacts? keywords: Claims History Pending Claims Insurance Claim Loss Run Report Claim Resolution Litigation Settlement hints: Frequent claims in a specific category (e.g. Cyber or Professional Liability) may indicate systemic operational weaknesses.

## Competitive & Market Risks

* Competitor Threat Analysis: Is there a matrix identifying key competitors and assessing their potential to disrupt the company's market share? keywords: Competitor Analysis Threat Matrix Market Disruption Vulnerability Assessment Competitive Risk Market Share Loss Rivalry Analysis hints: Check if the analysis includes "indirect competitors" or emerging startups that could pivot into the company's core space.  
* Supply Chain & Dependency Risks: Are risks associated with single-source suppliers or critical third-party service providers documented? keywords: Supply Chain Risk Single Source Supplier Critical Vendor Dependency Dependency Mapping Vendor Risk Procurement Risk Single Point of Failure hints: Identify dependencies on specific cloud providers or hardware manufacturers where a service interruption would halt operations.  
* Intellectual Property Risks: Is there an assessment of IP-related threats such as patent expirations or the emergence of patent trolls? keywords: IP Risk Patent Expiration Patent Troll Freedom to Operate FTO IP Litigation Infringement Risk IP Portfolio Assessment hints: Review "Freedom to Operate" (FTO) reports to ensure the company isn't unknowingly infringing on existing third-party patents.
