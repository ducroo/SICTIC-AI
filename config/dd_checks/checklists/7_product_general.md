# 7 Product-General

## Product Definition & Validation

### Product Scope & Current Readiness

Is the current product or service clearly specified, including its intended use, delivered functionality, development stage and known limitations?

Compare specifications, demonstrations and delivery records. Distinguish what is available today from prototypes, planned features and unsupported claims.

**Keywords:** product specification, service description, intended use, prototype, demonstration, release, development stage, limitations

### Performance & Repeatability

Is there documented testing or operational evidence that the product or service meets its stated performance and quality requirements under relevant conditions?

Identify the tested version, conditions, acceptance criteria, results and unresolved failures. Distinguish a single demonstration from repeatable delivery; assess evidence appropriate to the documented development stage.

**Keywords:** validation, test report, performance, acceptance criteria, pilot, repeatability, reliability, operational results

### Development Roadmap & Dependencies

Does the development roadmap identify the work, resources and dependencies needed to reach the next product or service milestone?

Reconcile proposed dates with unresolved technical or operational questions, validation work, supplier commitments and available resources. Separate completed work from assumptions requiring validation.

**Keywords:** product roadmap, development milestone, technical dependency, delivery plan, resources, validation plan, feasibility

## Intellectual Property & Third-Party Rights

### Ownership & Chain of Title

Does the company have documented ownership or sufficient rights to use and commercialize the assets essential to its product or service?

Review relevant assignments and licenses for inventions, software, designs, data, content and know-how, including work by founders, employees, contractors and research partners before incorporation. Identify material gaps without assuming that every business requires patents.

**Keywords:** IP assignment, ownership, chain of title, license, founder contribution, contractor, research partner, data rights, designs

### License Restrictions & Infringement Exposure

Do third-party licenses, supplier terms or disclosed IP disputes impose material restrictions on the intended product, markets or delivery model?

Identify relevant field-of-use, territory, exclusivity, redistribution and termination conditions, together with documented infringement concerns or clearance assessments. Distinguish documented restrictions from issues requiring specialist review; missing legal analysis does not establish infringement.

**Keywords:** third-party license, commercial rights, field of use, territory, exclusivity, open source, infringement, freedom to operate, IP dispute

### Confidentiality & Knowledge Retention

Are the company's material trade secrets, designs, records and operating know-how protected and accessible to the people responsible for delivery?

Assess relevant confidentiality agreements, access controls, documentation and handover arrangements. Identify reliance on knowledge or assets controlled solely by an individual or external partner, using the actual delivery model.

**Keywords:** trade secret, confidentiality, NDA, access control, documentation, knowledge transfer, handover, proprietary know-how

## Delivery, Production & Scaling

### Delivery Process & Control

Is there a documented, repeatable process for producing or delivering the product or service, with clear responsibilities and acceptance checks?

Trace the relevant design, sourcing, production or deployment steps through delivery and support. Assess controls appropriate to the documented stage and delivery model; distinguish working processes from planned arrangements.

**Keywords:** delivery process, production workflow, deployment, service operations, process documentation, acceptance, operating responsibility

### Critical Suppliers & Dependencies

Are critical suppliers, partners, platforms and inputs identified, with evidence of availability, commercial terms and practical alternatives where needed?

Assess concentration, lead times, capacity commitments, switching constraints and contingency arrangements. Include dependencies such as materials, contract production, cloud services or specialist service providers only where relevant to the business.

**Keywords:** supplier, partner, critical input, lead time, single source, platform dependency, contract manufacturer, cloud provider, contingency

### Capacity, Cost & Scale-Up

Is there evidence that the delivery or production model can reach the next planned volume while maintaining required quality, lead times and support levels?

Compare measured capacity, throughput, unit delivery costs and failure or rework rates with the scale-up plan. Identify bottlenecks and required investment; distinguish tested capacity from forecasts and avoid assuming that prototype costs represent scaled costs.

**Keywords:** capacity, throughput, scale-up, unit cost, production ramp, utilization, yield, rework, delivery time, support capacity

### Quality Control & Corrective Action

Are quality requirements, testing or inspection procedures, and the handling of defects or service failures documented and used in practice?

Review relevant acceptance records, complaints, returns, incident investigations and corrective actions. Identify unresolved issues affecting customer outcomes or repeatable delivery; missing records do not prove an absence of defects.

**Keywords:** quality control, testing, inspection, acceptance record, defects, complaints, returns, root cause, corrective action

## Safety, Compliance & Continuity

### Applicable Requirements & Approvals

Has the company identified the product- or service-specific requirements, approvals and contractual certifications relevant to its intended use and target markets?

Compare documented obligations with obtained approvals, their scope and any remaining work, timing or dependencies. Do not assume a sector-specific certification is mandatory for every business; distinguish documented inapplicability from missing evidence about applicability.

**Keywords:** regulatory requirements, approval, certification, intended use, target market, compliance roadmap, permit, audit scope

### Safety & Reliability Risks

Are material safety and reliability risks associated with the product or service identified and addressed through appropriate design, testing and operating measures?

Review documented hazards, failure modes, incidents and mitigations for the actual use conditions. Assess remaining validation work against the current stage; do not infer safety or reliability solely from an absence of reported incidents.

**Keywords:** safety, reliability, hazard analysis, failure mode, risk assessment, mitigation, incident, validation, operating conditions

### Data Protection & Security

Where the product or service uses sensitive information or digital systems, are the relevant data flows, access arrangements and protection measures documented and supported by evidence?

Assess the handling of customer and operational data, third-party access, retention and known security issues in the actual delivery model. State supported limits of applicability and unresolved evidence gaps without imposing a particular technology or certification.

**Keywords:** data flow, sensitive information, access control, data protection, retention, security, third-party processing, vulnerability

### Continuity, Recovery & Support

Are there practical arrangements to maintain or restore delivery and support if a critical system, supplier, facility or operating dependency fails?

Compare contingency, recovery and support arrangements with customer commitments and documented interruption risks. Look for evidence that the relevant arrangements are workable, rather than treating the existence of a written plan as proof of readiness.

**Keywords:** business continuity, recovery, backup, supplier disruption, facility outage, contingency plan, customer support, service commitment
