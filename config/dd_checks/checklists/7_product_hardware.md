# 7 Product-Hardware

## Intellectual Property & Intangible Assets

* Patent Portfolio Evidence: Is there a comprehensive register of all granted and pending patents including filing numbers and jurisdictions? keywords: Patent Register Filing Number Priority Date Patent Application Jurisdictions WIPO EPO USPTO Patent Portfolio hints: Verify that the company is listed as the current assignee for all patents and check for upcoming maintenance fee deadlines.  
* Trademark & Domain Assets: Is there documentation of all registered trademarks and domain names proving ownership and renewal dates? keywords: Trademark Registration Domain Ownership Renewal Date Brand Assets URL Portfolio WHOIS Proof Intellectual Property hints: Ensure all core brand domains are registered for at least 2 years ahead and that the registrant is the legal entity.  
* Freedom to Operate (FTO) & Clearance: Are there FTO reports or research confirming the ability to commercialize technology without infringing third-party IP? keywords: FTO Report Freedom to Operate Clearance Search Infringement Analysis IP Search Prior Art Legal Opinion Clearance Study hints: Look for specific risk areas or crowded spaces identified by legal counsel that could necessitate future design changes.  
* IP Assignment & Chain of Title: Are fully executed IP assignment agreements available from all founders employees and external consultants? keywords: IP Assignment Agreement Invention Assignment Intellectual Property Transfer Chain of Title Employee Contract Consultant IP Assignment hints: Check for a Contribution Agreement for work done prior to incorporation to ensure no founder-owned IP remains outside the company.  
* Patent Defensibility & Strength: Is there an assessment of the patentability and defensibility of core claims including prior art searches? keywords: Patentability Assessment Core Claims Defensibility Analysis Prior Art Search Novelty Report Validity Opinion Patent Strength hints: Evaluate the strength of Independent Claims versus Dependent Claims to understand the difficulty of competitors designing around the patent.  
* Infringement & Litigation Risk: Has the company disclosed all past pending or threatened litigation regarding IP infringement? keywords: IP Litigation Cease and Desist Infringement Claim Legal Dispute Demand Letter Patent Dispute Lawsuit Infringement Risk hints: Specifically check for any Cease and Desist letters received in the last 24 months that have not been formally resolved.  
* IP Strategy & Roadmap: Is there a strategic document outlining the future filing strategy and international expansion plans? keywords: IP Strategy Patent Roadmap Filing Strategy Defensive Strategy International IP PCT Filing Strategy Intellectual Property Roadmap hints: Align the IP roadmap with the geographic Go-to-Market strategy to ensure protection exists in all target sales regions.  
* Trade Secret Protections: Are there protocols for trade secret protection including access control and confidentiality management? keywords: Trade Secret Confidentiality Protocol Access Control NDA Proprietary Know-how Confidential Information Data Security internal protocols hints: Look for least-privilege access logs or partitioned documentation folders that restrict sensitive engineering data to authorized personnel.

## Hardware Engineering & Design

* System Architecture & Specifications: Are there detailed Technical Design Documents (TDD) and system architecture diagrams for the current hardware? keywords: TDD Technical Design Document System Architecture Block Diagram Engineering Spec Hardware Design Schematics Technical Architecture hints: Ensure diagrams include all subsystems like power and connectivity and match the actual functionality of the current prototype.  
* Design Rationale & Trade-offs: Is there evidence of design-choice analysis covering power management component selection and performance trade-offs? keywords: Design Rationale Trade-off Analysis Component Selection Power Management Performance Evaluation Engineering Log Design Choices hints: Look for Decision Matrices or Simulations that justify why certain expensive components or power-intensive chips were chosen.  
* Firmware Lifecycle & Deployment: Is the firmware development process documented including version control security and OTA update protocols? keywords: Firmware Lifecycle Version Control GitHub GitLab Bootloader Security OTA Update Firmware Deployment Code Review Firmware Security hints: Verify that Over-the-Air (OTA) updates include encryption and digital signatures to prevent malicious code injection.  
* Open Source & Third-Party Software (S-BOM): Is there a complete Software Bill of Materials (S-BOM) identifying all open-source and third-party code? keywords: S-BOM Software Bill of Materials Open Source Library OSS Compliance Third Party Software License Audit Software Inventory hints: Check for Copyleft licenses like GPL that might require the company to open-source its proprietary firmware.

## Manufacturing & Supply Chain

* Comprehensive Bill of Materials (BOM): Is there a granular BOM list including manufacturer part numbers (MPNs) cost-per-unit (CPU) and single-source flagging? keywords: BOM Bill of Materials MPN Part Number Cost-per-unit CPU Single Source Component List Inventory Spec Product Cost hints: Flag any Single Source components with lead times longer than 12 weeks as a significant operational risk.  
* Custom-Fabricated Componentry: Are CAD files and specifications provided for custom components proving ownership of molds and tooling? keywords: CAD Files Tooling Mold Ownership Custom Component PCB Layout Gerber Files 3D Model STEP File Fabricated Parts hints: Explicitly check the Tooling Agreements to ensure the company—not the manufacturer—legally owns the injection molds.  
* Lead Time & Supply Chain Resilience: Is there a lead time analysis for critical components including contingency plans for shortages? keywords: Lead Time Analysis Supply Chain Resilience Shortage Contingency Buffer Stock Safety Stock Vendor Diversification Supply Chain Risk hints: Verify the Safety Stock levels for high-risk components and compare them against the projected production ramp-up.  
* Vendor & Supplier Ecosystem: Is there a register of primary and secondary suppliers including performance data and credit terms? keywords: Supplier List Vendor Register Secondary Supplier Approved Vendor List AVL Credit Terms Supplier Performance Vendor Ecosystem hints: Ensure that every Critical component has at least one vetted Alternate Vendor (AVL) in the documentation.  
* Contract Manufacturing (CM) Agreements: Are there signed agreements with assembly partners including MSAs and SOWs? keywords: Contract Manufacturer CM Agreement MSA Master Service Agreement SOW Assembly Partner Manufacturing Contract CM Relationship hints: Check the Yield Rate and Liability clauses to see who pays for scrap or defective units during the production run.  
* Manufacturing Process Flow: Is there a detailed description of the production workflow from SMT to final assembly? keywords: Manufacturing Process Production Flow SMT Surface Mount Technology Assembly Workflow QA Inspection Packaging Process Map hints: Look for Process Control steps like AOI or ICT to ensure quality is being checked at each major stage of fabrication.

## Quality, Testing & Compliance

* Validation Testing (DVT/PVT): Are Design Validation Testing (DVT) and Production Validation Testing (PVT) results available with yield rates? keywords: DVT PVT Design Validation Production Validation Yield Rate Failure Analysis Test Report Quality Log Reliability Testing hints: Compare the Initial Yield vs Target Yield to identify if the product design is actually ready for mass-scale manufacturing.  
* Regulatory & Security Certifications: Is there evidence of obtained certifications or a documented roadmap for upcoming regulatory filings? keywords: Certification CE FCC RoHS REACH ISO 9001 ISO 27001 Regulatory Roadmap Compliance Certificate Safety Standards hints: For medical or safety-critical hardware verify that the Certification Lab reports are signed and match the latest product revision.
