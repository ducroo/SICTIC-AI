# 7 Product-Hardware

## Intellectual Property & Intangible Assets

### Patent Portfolio Evidence

Is there a comprehensive register of all granted and pending patents including filing numbers and jurisdictions?

Verify that the company is listed as the current assignee for all patents and check for upcoming maintenance fee deadlines.

**Keywords:** Patent Register Filing Number Priority Date Patent Application Jurisdictions WIPO EPO USPTO Patent Portfolio

### Trademark & Domain Assets

Is there documentation of all registered trademarks and domain names proving ownership and renewal dates?

Ensure all core brand domains are registered for at least 2 years ahead and that the registrant is the legal entity.

**Keywords:** Trademark Registration Domain Ownership Renewal Date Brand Assets URL Portfolio WHOIS Proof Intellectual Property

### Freedom to Operate (FTO) & Clearance

Are there FTO reports or research confirming the ability to commercialize technology without infringing third-party IP?

Look for specific risk areas or crowded spaces identified by legal counsel that could necessitate future design changes.

**Keywords:** FTO Report Freedom to Operate Clearance Search Infringement Analysis IP Search Prior Art Legal Opinion Clearance Study

### IP Assignment & Chain of Title

Are fully executed IP assignment agreements available from all founders employees and external consultants?

Check for a Contribution Agreement for work done prior to incorporation to ensure no founder-owned IP remains outside the company.

**Keywords:** IP Assignment Agreement Invention Assignment Intellectual Property Transfer Chain of Title Employee Contract Consultant IP Assignment

### Patent Defensibility & Strength

Is there an assessment of the patentability and defensibility of core claims including prior art searches?

Evaluate the strength of Independent Claims versus Dependent Claims to understand the difficulty of competitors designing around the patent.

**Keywords:** Patentability Assessment Core Claims Defensibility Analysis Prior Art Search Novelty Report Validity Opinion Patent Strength

### Infringement & Litigation Risk

Has the company disclosed all past pending or threatened litigation regarding IP infringement?

Specifically check for any Cease and Desist letters received in the last 24 months that have not been formally resolved.

**Keywords:** IP Litigation Cease and Desist Infringement Claim Legal Dispute Demand Letter Patent Dispute Lawsuit Infringement Risk

### IP Strategy & Roadmap

Is there a strategic document outlining the future filing strategy and international expansion plans?

Align the IP roadmap with the geographic Go-to-Market strategy to ensure protection exists in all target sales regions.

**Keywords:** IP Strategy Patent Roadmap Filing Strategy Defensive Strategy International IP PCT Filing Strategy Intellectual Property Roadmap

### Trade Secret Protections

Are there protocols for trade secret protection including access control and confidentiality management?

Look for least-privilege access logs or partitioned documentation folders that restrict sensitive engineering data to authorized personnel.

**Keywords:** Trade Secret Confidentiality Protocol Access Control NDA Proprietary Know-how Confidential Information Data Security internal protocols

## Hardware Engineering & Design

### System Architecture & Specifications

Are there detailed Technical Design Documents (TDD) and system architecture diagrams for the current hardware?

Ensure diagrams include all subsystems like power and connectivity and match the actual functionality of the current prototype.

**Keywords:** TDD Technical Design Document System Architecture Block Diagram Engineering Spec Hardware Design Schematics Technical Architecture

### Design Rationale & Trade-offs

Is there evidence of design-choice analysis covering power management component selection and performance trade-offs?

Look for Decision Matrices or Simulations that justify why certain expensive components or power-intensive chips were chosen.

**Keywords:** Design Rationale Trade-off Analysis Component Selection Power Management Performance Evaluation Engineering Log Design Choices

### Firmware Lifecycle & Deployment

Is the firmware development process documented including version control security and OTA update protocols?

Verify that Over-the-Air (OTA) updates include encryption and digital signatures to prevent malicious code injection.

**Keywords:** Firmware Lifecycle Version Control GitHub GitLab Bootloader Security OTA Update Firmware Deployment Code Review Firmware Security

### Open Source & Third-Party Software (S-BOM)

Is there a complete Software Bill of Materials (S-BOM) identifying all open-source and third-party code?

Check for Copyleft licenses like GPL that might require the company to open-source its proprietary firmware.

**Keywords:** S-BOM Software Bill of Materials Open Source Library OSS Compliance Third Party Software License Audit Software Inventory

## Manufacturing & Supply Chain

### Comprehensive Bill of Materials (BOM)

Is there a granular BOM list including manufacturer part numbers (MPNs) cost-per-unit (CPU) and single-source flagging?

Flag any Single Source components with lead times longer than 12 weeks as a significant operational risk.

**Keywords:** BOM Bill of Materials MPN Part Number Cost-per-unit CPU Single Source Component List Inventory Spec Product Cost

### Custom-Fabricated Componentry

Are CAD files and specifications provided for custom components proving ownership of molds and tooling?

Explicitly check the Tooling Agreements to ensure the company—not the manufacturer—legally owns the injection molds.

**Keywords:** CAD Files Tooling Mold Ownership Custom Component PCB Layout Gerber Files 3D Model STEP File Fabricated Parts

### Lead Time & Supply Chain Resilience

Is there a lead time analysis for critical components including contingency plans for shortages?

Verify the Safety Stock levels for high-risk components and compare them against the projected production ramp-up.

**Keywords:** Lead Time Analysis Supply Chain Resilience Shortage Contingency Buffer Stock Safety Stock Vendor Diversification Supply Chain Risk

### Vendor & Supplier Ecosystem

Is there a register of primary and secondary suppliers including performance data and credit terms?

Ensure that every Critical component has at least one vetted Alternate Vendor (AVL) in the documentation.

**Keywords:** Supplier List Vendor Register Secondary Supplier Approved Vendor List AVL Credit Terms Supplier Performance Vendor Ecosystem

### Contract Manufacturing (CM) Agreements

Are there signed agreements with assembly partners including MSAs and SOWs?

Check the Yield Rate and Liability clauses to see who pays for scrap or defective units during the production run.

**Keywords:** Contract Manufacturer CM Agreement MSA Master Service Agreement SOW Assembly Partner Manufacturing Contract CM Relationship

### Manufacturing Process Flow

Is there a detailed description of the production workflow from SMT to final assembly?

Look for Process Control steps like AOI or ICT to ensure quality is being checked at each major stage of fabrication.

**Keywords:** Manufacturing Process Production Flow SMT Surface Mount Technology Assembly Workflow QA Inspection Packaging Process Map

## Quality, Testing & Compliance

### Validation Testing (DVT/PVT)

Are Design Validation Testing (DVT) and Production Validation Testing (PVT) results available with yield rates?

Compare the Initial Yield vs Target Yield to identify if the product design is actually ready for mass-scale manufacturing.

**Keywords:** DVT PVT Design Validation Production Validation Yield Rate Failure Analysis Test Report Quality Log Reliability Testing

### Regulatory & Security Certifications

Is there evidence of obtained certifications or a documented roadmap for upcoming regulatory filings?

For medical or safety-critical hardware verify that the Certification Lab reports are signed and match the latest product revision.

**Keywords:** Certification CE FCC RoHS REACH ISO 9001 ISO 27001 Regulatory Roadmap Compliance Certificate Safety Standards
