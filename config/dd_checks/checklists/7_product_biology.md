# 7 Product-Biology & Life Sciences

## Intellectual Property & Scientific Freedom

* Patent Portfolio & Licensing: Is there a comprehensive register of patents and exclusive licenses especially university spin-off agreements or inter-institutional agreements? keywords: Patent PCT Exclusive License IP Assignment University Spin-off Tech Transfer Field of Use Freedom to Operate hints: Carefully check "Field of Use" and "Territory" restrictions in university licenses to ensure the startup has the right to commercialize the specific therapeutic or diagnostic.  
* Freedom to Operate (FTO) & Prior Art: Are there FTO reports or legal opinions confirming the ability to commercialize the biological asset without infringing third-party IP? keywords: FTO Freedom to Operate Patent Landscape Prior Art Search Clearance Opinion Infringement Analysis White Space Analysis hints: In Biotech FTO is often "qualified" by pending broad patents; look for specific "work-around" strategies for known dominant patents in the therapeutic area.  
* Lab Notebooks & Data Ownership: Is there evidence of rigorous data recording such as Electronic Lab Notebooks (ELN) that prove the chain of invention? keywords: ELN Lab Notebook Data Integrity Raw Data Invention Date Witnessed Notebook Data Ownership Scientific Audit hints: Ensure all lab data generated during the university phase or with external CROs has been formally assigned to the company.

## Scientific Validation & De-risking (Proof of Concept)

* Mechanism of Action (MoA) Validation: Is there experimental evidence (In Vitro or In Silico) proving the core biological mechanism of action? keywords: MoA Mechanism of Action Target Validation Assay Result Binding Affinity Proof of Concept PoC Biological Rationale hints: Look for "Dose-Response" curves; a lack of clear dose-dependency in early assays is a major red flag for the underlying biological hypothesis.  
* Pre-clinical Efficacy (In Vivo): Does the data room contain results from animal models or organ-on-a-chip simulations that demonstrate efficacy? keywords: In Vivo Animal Model Xenograft Efficacy Study Disease Model Pre-clinical Data Statistical Significance P-value hints: Check if the animal models used are industry-standard for the specific indication; non-standard models may require expensive re-validation later.  
* Reproducibility & Internal Controls: Is there documentation showing that the core results have been reproduced across different batches or by independent team members? keywords: Reproducibility Internal Validation Control Group Batch Consistency Assay Sensitivity Specificity Validation Report hints: Flag any "Key Results" that rely on a single experiment without clear negative and positive controls.

## Toxicity, Safety & ADME

* Preliminary Toxicology & Cytotoxicity: Have preliminary toxicity screens (e.g. cell viability assays) been conducted to identify immediate safety risks? keywords: Toxicology Tox Screen Cytotoxicity Cell Viability Safety Profile LD50 Acute Toxicity Off-target Effects hints: High cytotoxicity in primary cell cultures at therapeutic concentrations suggests a narrow "Safety Window" and high risk of future clinical failure.  
* ADME & Pharmacokinetics (PK/PD): Are there early studies on Absorption Distribution Metabolism and Excretion (ADME) to predict how the lead candidate behaves in a living system? keywords: ADME PK/PD Pharmacokinetics Pharmacodynamics Half-life Bioavailability Metabolism Clearance Distribution Profile hints: Look for "Metabolic Stability" data; if the candidate degrades too quickly it may never reach the target organ in a human subject.  
* Off-target Profiling: Is there evidence of screening against common off-target receptors (e.g. hERG channel) to rule out systemic side effects? keywords: Off-target Screen hERG Binding Safety Pharmacology Profiling Cross-reactivity Specificity Testing hints: Early identification of "hERG binding" or "Cardiotoxicity" risk is a critical de-risking step that prevents mid-stage clinical shutdowns.

## Regulatory Strategy & Clinical Path

* Target Product Profile (TPP): Does the company have a clear TPP defining the clinical end-point and competitive differentiation against the current Standard of Care (SoC)? keywords: TPP Target Product Profile Clinical End-point Standard of Care SoC Indication Unmet Need Competitive Differentiation hints: Evaluate if the TPP "Minimum Acceptable Result" is actually competitive enough to gain market share from existing treatments.  
* Regulatory Roadmap & IND-enabling Plan: Is there a documented strategy for pre-clinical development and the eventual Investigational New Drug (IND) or CTA filing? keywords: Regulatory Roadmap IND CTA FDA EMA Pre-clinical Strategy GLP Studies Clinical Development Plan hints: Identify the specific "IND-enabling studies" (e.g. GLP Tox) required and verify if the timeline and budget in the financial plan are realistic for these high-cost items.  
* Manufacturing & CMC Strategy: Is there a preliminary plan for Chemistry Manufacturing and Controls (CMC) including scale-up and purity standards? keywords: CMC Purity Scale-up Formulation Stability Testing Batch Record CMO CDMO Manufacturing Process hints: For biologics or cell therapies "The Process is the Product"; any lack of a scalable manufacturing plan is a significant long-term valuation risk.
