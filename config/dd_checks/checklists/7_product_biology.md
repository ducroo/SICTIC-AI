# 7 Product-Biology & Life Sciences

## Intellectual Property & Scientific Freedom

### Patent Portfolio & Licensing

Is there a comprehensive register of patents and exclusive licenses especially university spin-off agreements or inter-institutional agreements?

Carefully check "Field of Use" and "Territory" restrictions in university licenses to ensure the startup has the right to commercialize the specific therapeutic or diagnostic.

**Keywords:** Patent PCT Exclusive License IP Assignment University Spin-off Tech Transfer Field of Use Freedom to Operate

### Freedom to Operate (FTO) & Prior Art

Are there FTO reports or legal opinions confirming the ability to commercialize the biological asset without infringing third-party IP?

In Biotech FTO is often "qualified" by pending broad patents; look for specific "work-around" strategies for known dominant patents in the therapeutic area.

**Keywords:** FTO Freedom to Operate Patent Landscape Prior Art Search Clearance Opinion Infringement Analysis White Space Analysis

### Lab Notebooks & Data Ownership

Is there evidence of rigorous data recording such as Electronic Lab Notebooks (ELN) that prove the chain of invention?

Ensure all lab data generated during the university phase or with external CROs has been formally assigned to the company.

**Keywords:** ELN Lab Notebook Data Integrity Raw Data Invention Date Witnessed Notebook Data Ownership Scientific Audit

## Scientific Validation & De-risking (Proof of Concept)

### Mechanism of Action (MoA) Validation

Is there experimental evidence (In Vitro or In Silico) proving the core biological mechanism of action?

Look for "Dose-Response" curves; a lack of clear dose-dependency in early assays is a major red flag for the underlying biological hypothesis.

**Keywords:** MoA Mechanism of Action Target Validation Assay Result Binding Affinity Proof of Concept PoC Biological Rationale

### Pre-clinical Efficacy (In Vivo)

Does the data room contain results from animal models or organ-on-a-chip simulations that demonstrate efficacy?

Check if the animal models used are industry-standard for the specific indication; non-standard models may require expensive re-validation later.

**Keywords:** In Vivo Animal Model Xenograft Efficacy Study Disease Model Pre-clinical Data Statistical Significance P-value

### Reproducibility & Internal Controls

Is there documentation showing that the core results have been reproduced across different batches or by independent team members?

Flag any "Key Results" that rely on a single experiment without clear negative and positive controls.

**Keywords:** Reproducibility Internal Validation Control Group Batch Consistency Assay Sensitivity Specificity Validation Report

## Toxicity, Safety & ADME

### Preliminary Toxicology & Cytotoxicity

Have preliminary toxicity screens (e.g. cell viability assays) been conducted to identify immediate safety risks?

High cytotoxicity in primary cell cultures at therapeutic concentrations suggests a narrow "Safety Window" and high risk of future clinical failure.

**Keywords:** Toxicology Tox Screen Cytotoxicity Cell Viability Safety Profile LD50 Acute Toxicity Off-target Effects

### ADME & Pharmacokinetics (PK/PD)

Are there early studies on Absorption Distribution Metabolism and Excretion (ADME) to predict how the lead candidate behaves in a living system?

Look for "Metabolic Stability" data; if the candidate degrades too quickly it may never reach the target organ in a human subject.

**Keywords:** ADME PK/PD Pharmacokinetics Pharmacodynamics Half-life Bioavailability Metabolism Clearance Distribution Profile

### Off-target Profiling

Is there evidence of screening against common off-target receptors (e.g. hERG channel) to rule out systemic side effects?

Early identification of "hERG binding" or "Cardiotoxicity" risk is a critical de-risking step that prevents mid-stage clinical shutdowns.

**Keywords:** Off-target Screen hERG Binding Safety Pharmacology Profiling Cross-reactivity Specificity Testing

## Regulatory Strategy & Clinical Path

### Target Product Profile (TPP)

Does the company have a clear TPP defining the clinical end-point and competitive differentiation against the current Standard of Care (SoC)?

Evaluate if the TPP "Minimum Acceptable Result" is actually competitive enough to gain market share from existing treatments.

**Keywords:** TPP Target Product Profile Clinical End-point Standard of Care SoC Indication Unmet Need Competitive Differentiation

### Regulatory Roadmap & IND-enabling Plan

Is there a documented strategy for pre-clinical development and the eventual Investigational New Drug (IND) or CTA filing?

Identify the specific "IND-enabling studies" (e.g. GLP Tox) required and verify if the timeline and budget in the financial plan are realistic for these high-cost items.

**Keywords:** Regulatory Roadmap IND CTA FDA EMA Pre-clinical Strategy GLP Studies Clinical Development Plan

### Manufacturing & CMC Strategy

Is there a preliminary plan for Chemistry Manufacturing and Controls (CMC) including scale-up and purity standards?

For biologics or cell therapies "The Process is the Product"; any lack of a scalable manufacturing plan is a significant long-term valuation risk.

**Keywords:** CMC Purity Scale-up Formulation Stability Testing Batch Record CMO CDMO Manufacturing Process
