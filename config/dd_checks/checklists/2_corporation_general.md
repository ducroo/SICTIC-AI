# 2 Corporation-General

## Corporate Governance & Structure

* Entity Mapping: Is there clear documentation of the corporate structure? Keywords: corporate structure, subsidiaries, associated entities, domestic, international, organizational chart, group structure. Hint: Check for documentation confirming the legal relationship between all related parties.  
* Ownership Evidence: Are current Commercial Register excerpts available for all legal entities? Keywords: Commercial Register, Handelsregisterauszüge, excerpts, legal entities, ownership records. Hint: Ensure all provided excerpts are current and cover the reporting period.  
* Credit Standing: Is a recent Debt Collection Register excerpt available? Keywords: Debt Collection Register, Betreibungsauszug, credit report, financial standing, last 3 months. Hint: Verify the date of the provided excerpt falls within the specified timeframe.  
* Governing Documents: Are signed Articles of Association and Deed of Incorporation present? Keywords: Articles of Association, Deed of Incorporation, bylaws, founding documents, corporate charter. Hint: Verify all copies are fully executed and dated.  
* Signing Authority Policy: Is there evidence of a formal signature policy? Keywords: signature policy, Organisationsreglement, collective signature, signing authority, Commercial Register, mandate. Hint: The policy must explicitly match the current signatory requirements registered publicly.  
* Corporate Memory: Are signed minutes available for key meetings? Keywords: Board minutes, Board of Directors, General Meetings of Shareholders, board resolutions, meeting minutes, incorporation date. Hint: Check for signed attendance lists alongside the meeting minutes.

## Capital & Shareholder Relations

* Equity Accuracy: Does a signed Register of Shareholders reconcile with the Cap Table? Keywords: Aktionsregister, Register of Shareholders, Cap Table, share ledger, unvested stock, employee options, equity structure, shareholder record. Hint: Ensure the reconciliation accounts for all grants, exercised, and unvested instruments.  
* Shareholder Governance: Is a Shareholders’ Agreement (SHA) available and signed by all current shareholders? Keywords: Shareholders’ Agreement, SHA, shareholder rights, governance agreement, buy-sell provisions. Hint: Confirm all signatories listed in the agreement are current legal owners.  
* Dilution Instruments: Are all outstanding dilution instruments disclosed? Keywords: CLA, Warrants, Convertible Notes, SAFEs, convertible debt, equity instruments, conversion triggers, cap table summary. Hint: Provide a detailed summary table showing the conversion terms and potential dilution impact.  
* Audit History: Are reports or results from prior due diligence cycles available? Keywords: due diligence, prior audit, diligence reports, historical reviews, external reports, transaction reviews. Hint: Provide context on the scope and findings of the historical reviews.

## Financial, Tax & Social Compliance

* Social Security Standing: Are official certificates of standing confirming paid contributions available? Keywords: Ausgleichskasse, AHV, IV, EO, ALV, social security contributions, mandatory contributions, state compliance. Hint: Verify the certificates are up-to-date and confirm zero outstanding liabilities.  
* Insurance Coverage: Is evidence of active, adequate insurance coverage available? Keywords: UVG, Unfallversicherung (Accident), Betriebshaftpflicht (Liability), BVG, pension fund, insurance policy, coverage letters. Hint: Check expiration dates and confirm policy limits meet industry standards.  
* ESOP Tax Ruling: Is a formal Tax Ruling regarding equity taxation available? Keywords: ESOP, Employee Stock Option Plan, PSOP, Phantom Stock Option Plan, share participation schemes, tax ruling, valuation, tax authority correspondence. Hint: Confirm the ruling covers the specific structure of the incentive scheme.  
* General Tax Correspondence: Is all material tax correspondence available? Keywords: tax authority, Swiss tax, international tax, tax rulings, correspondence, tax advisory letters. Hint: Do not rely solely on general summaries; provide the underlying communication.

## Contracts & Legal Exposure

* Material Agreements: Are all active commercial contracts (ie. larger than CHF 20,000) with significant value provided? Keywords: commercial contracts, material agreements, annual value, client contracts, vendor agreements. Hint: Include renewal dates, termination clauses, and any required third-party consents.  
* External Services: Are signed agreements with professional service providers present? Keywords: Accounting counsel, Tax counsel, Legal counsel, external advisors, service agreements, retainer agreements. Hint: Verify that the scope of work and confidentiality clauses are current.  
* Litigation Status: Is there a formal declaration of legal exposure? Keywords: litigation, legal claims, pending lawsuits, threatened actions, legal opinions, defense counsel letters. Hint: Require input from internal management and external legal counsel.

## Intellectual Property

* Patent Ownership and Inventions: Does the company hold full legal title to all patents and pending inventions, and have all "Service Inventions" been properly claimed? Keywords: Patent assignment (Zession), IPI/IGE registration, Swiss Patent Act (PatA), Art. 332 CO (OR), inventor remuneration, PCT filings. Hint: Audit IPI register extracts and ensure signed "Invention Assignment Declarations" (Inventionsabtretungserklärungen) exist for all R\&D personnel to confirm compliance with Art. 332 of the Swiss Code of Obligations.  
* Brand and Digital Assets: Are all trademarks, .ch domains, and digital assets legally registered to or owned by the Swiss entity rather than individuals? Keywords: Swiss Trademark (Markenschutz), IGE/IPI registration, .ch domain holder, Swiss Copyright Act (URG/LDA), software usage rights, transfer of title. Hint: Verify registration records at the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IGE) and ensure WHOIS data for domains matches the company's Swiss commercial registry (UID) entry.  
* Personnel IP Transfer: Have all IP rights from employees and external contractors been validly transferred to the company under Swiss law? Keywords: Swiss Code of Obligations (CO/OR), Art. 332 OR, employment contract (Arbeitsvertrag), mandate agreement (Auftrag), moral rights waiver, copyright assignment. Hint: For employees, verify the "Scope of Work" aligns with automatic transfer under Art. 332 OR; for external contractors, ensure a written "Mandate Agreement" contains an explicit, broad transfer of all IP and usage rights (Nutzungsrechte).
