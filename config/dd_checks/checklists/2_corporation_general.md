# 2 Corporation-General

## Corporate Governance & Structure

### Entity Mapping

Is there clear documentation of the corporate structure?

Check for documentation confirming the legal relationship between all related parties.

**Keywords:** corporate structure, subsidiaries, associated entities, domestic, international, organizational chart, group structure.

### Ownership Evidence

Are current Commercial Register excerpts available for all legal entities?

Ensure all provided excerpts are current and cover the reporting period.

**Keywords:** Commercial Register, Handelsregisterauszüge, excerpts, legal entities, ownership records.

### Credit Standing

Is a recent Debt Collection Register excerpt available?

Verify the date of the provided excerpt falls within the specified timeframe.

**Keywords:** Debt Collection Register, Betreibungsauszug, credit report, financial standing, last 3 months.

### Governing Documents

Are signed Articles of Association and Deed of Incorporation present?

Verify all copies are fully executed and dated.

**Keywords:** Articles of Association, Deed of Incorporation, bylaws, founding documents, corporate charter.

### Signing Authority Policy

Is there evidence of a formal signature policy?

The policy must explicitly match the current signatory requirements registered publicly.

**Keywords:** signature policy, Organisationsreglement, collective signature, signing authority, Commercial Register, mandate.

### Corporate Memory

Are signed minutes available for key meetings?

Check for signed attendance lists alongside the meeting minutes.

**Keywords:** Board minutes, Board of Directors, General Meetings of Shareholders, board resolutions, meeting minutes, incorporation date.

## Capital & Shareholder Relations

### Equity Accuracy

Does a signed Register of Shareholders reconcile with the Cap Table?

Ensure the reconciliation accounts for all grants, exercised, and unvested instruments.

**Keywords:** Aktionsregister, Register of Shareholders, Cap Table, share ledger, unvested stock, employee options, equity structure, shareholder record.

### Shareholder Governance

Is a Shareholders’ Agreement (SHA) available and signed by all current shareholders?

Confirm all signatories listed in the agreement are current legal owners.

**Keywords:** Shareholders’ Agreement, SHA, shareholder rights, governance agreement, buy-sell provisions.

### Dilution Instruments

Are all outstanding dilution instruments disclosed?

Provide a detailed summary table showing the conversion terms and potential dilution impact.

**Keywords:** CLA, Warrants, Convertible Notes, SAFEs, convertible debt, equity instruments, conversion triggers, cap table summary.

### Audit History

Are reports or results from prior due diligence cycles available?

Provide context on the scope and findings of the historical reviews.

**Keywords:** due diligence, prior audit, diligence reports, historical reviews, external reports, transaction reviews.

## Financial, Tax & Social Compliance

### Social Security Standing

Are official certificates of standing confirming paid contributions available?

Verify the certificates are up-to-date and confirm zero outstanding liabilities.

**Keywords:** Ausgleichskasse, AHV, IV, EO, ALV, social security contributions, mandatory contributions, state compliance.

### Insurance Coverage

Is evidence of active, adequate insurance coverage available?

Check expiration dates and confirm policy limits meet industry standards.

**Keywords:** UVG, Unfallversicherung (Accident), Betriebshaftpflicht (Liability), BVG, pension fund, insurance policy, coverage letters.

### ESOP Tax Ruling

Is a formal Tax Ruling regarding equity taxation available?

Confirm the ruling covers the specific structure of the incentive scheme.

**Keywords:** ESOP, Employee Stock Option Plan, PSOP, Phantom Stock Option Plan, share participation schemes, tax ruling, valuation, tax authority correspondence.

### General Tax Correspondence

Is all material tax correspondence available?

Do not rely solely on general summaries; provide the underlying communication.

**Keywords:** tax authority, Swiss tax, international tax, tax rulings, correspondence, tax advisory letters.

## Contracts & Legal Exposure

### Material Agreements

Are all active commercial contracts (ie. larger than CHF 20,000) with significant value provided?

Include renewal dates, termination clauses, and any required third-party consents.

**Keywords:** commercial contracts, material agreements, annual value, client contracts, vendor agreements.

### External Services

Are signed agreements with professional service providers present?

Verify that the scope of work and confidentiality clauses are current.

**Keywords:** Accounting counsel, Tax counsel, Legal counsel, external advisors, service agreements, retainer agreements.

### Litigation Status

Is there a formal declaration of legal exposure?

Require input from internal management and external legal counsel.

**Keywords:** litigation, legal claims, pending lawsuits, threatened actions, legal opinions, defense counsel letters.

## Intellectual Property

### Patent Ownership and Inventions

Does the company hold full legal title to all patents and pending inventions, and have all "Service Inventions" been properly claimed?

Audit IPI register extracts and ensure signed "Invention Assignment Declarations" (Inventionsabtretungserklärungen) exist for all R\&D personnel to confirm compliance with Art. 332 of the Swiss Code of Obligations.

**Keywords:** Patent assignment (Zession), IPI/IGE registration, Swiss Patent Act (PatA), Art. 332 CO (OR), inventor remuneration, PCT filings.

### Brand and Digital Assets

Are all trademarks, .ch domains, and digital assets legally registered to or owned by the Swiss entity rather than individuals?

Verify registration records at the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IGE) and ensure WHOIS data for domains matches the company's Swiss commercial registry (UID) entry.

**Keywords:** Swiss Trademark (Markenschutz), IGE/IPI registration, .ch domain holder, Swiss Copyright Act (URG/LDA), software usage rights, transfer of title.

### Personnel IP Transfer

Have all IP rights from employees and external contractors been validly transferred to the company under Swiss law?

For employees, verify the "Scope of Work" aligns with automatic transfer under Art. 332 OR; for external contractors, ensure a written "Mandate Agreement" contains an explicit, broad transfer of all IP and usage rights (Nutzungsrechte).

**Keywords:** Swiss Code of Obligations (CO/OR), Art. 332 OR, employment contract (Arbeitsvertrag), mandate agreement (Auftrag), moral rights waiver, copyright assignment.
