# 5 Corroborating Evidence

## Corroborating Evidence — Does Practice Match the SHA?

### Share Register Reconciliation

Does a signed Register of Shareholders reconcile exactly with the SHA's stated cap table, including unvested and unexercised options, with a clean chain of share and option transactions?

**Keywords:** Register of Shareholders, cap table, unvested options, unexercised options, share transaction, option transaction, reconciliation.

### Board Approval Compliance

Are signed minutes available for all Board meetings since the SHA was signed, showing that Important Board Matters were voted on and approved as required?

**Keywords:** signed board minutes, Important Board Matters, board approval, vote, SHA compliance, meeting record.

### Shareholder Approval Compliance

Are signed protocols available for all General Meetings since the SHA was signed, confirming that Important Shareholder Matters met the required quorum and majority?

**Keywords:** General Meeting protocol, shareholder minutes, Important Shareholder Matters, quorum, majority, approval compliance.

### Dilutive Instrument Reconciliation

Is there a complete summary of warrants, convertible notes, SAFEs, CLAs, options, and other equity-entitling instruments, including conversion triggers and dilution, reconciled against the SHA's anti-dilution and pro rata rights?

**Keywords:** warrants, convertible notes, SAFE, CLA, option grant, conversion trigger, dilution, anti-dilution, pro rata.

### Prior Due Diligence Findings

Are prior investors' due-diligence findings available, and do they identify SHA-related issues such as undisclosed side letters, governance breaches, or unresolved disputes?

**Keywords:** prior due diligence, investor findings, side letter, governance breach, unresolved dispute, SHA issue, diligence report.
