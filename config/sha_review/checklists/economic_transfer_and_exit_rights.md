# 3 Economic, Transfer and Exit Rights

## Economic Rights — Dividends & Liquidation Preference

### Dividend Policy

Does the SHA set a dividend policy, or is distribution left entirely to General Meeting discretion?

**Keywords:** dividend policy, distribution, General Meeting, shareholder resolution, retained earnings, dividend discretion.

### Initial Dividend Restriction

If PE or VC investors are involved, are dividends restricted for an initial period, and is that period clearly defined rather than left as a vague future catch-up?

**Keywords:** dividend restriction, PE investor, VC investor, initial period, catch-up dividend, distribution restriction.

### Dividend and Liquidation Separation

Is dividend preference clearly separated from liquidation preference?

**Keywords:** dividend preference, liquidation preference, preferred return, distribution waterfall, distinction.

### Liquidation Preference Structure

What is the liquidation-preference multiple, and is it fully participating, non-participating, or participating with a cap?

**Keywords:** liquidation preference, preference multiple, 1x, participating, non-participating, double dip, capped participation.

### Participation Cap and Conversion

If participation is capped, is the cap multiple stated with a conversion-to-common option once proceeds exceed it?

**Keywords:** participation cap, cap multiple, conversion to common, preferred conversion, exit proceeds, liquidation waterfall.

### Preference Seniority and Stacking

Is the seniority and stacking order clear across multiple preferred rounds, including whether the most recent round ranks senior and is paid first?

**Keywords:** seniority, liquidation stack, preferred rounds, pari passu, senior preferred, payment priority.

### IPO Preference Termination

Do liquidation and dividend preferences explicitly terminate upon an IPO?

**Keywords:** IPO, preference termination, liquidation preference, dividend preference, automatic conversion, public offering.

### Stage-Appropriate Preference Terms

Are the preference terms unusually harsh for the company's stage, potentially indicating down-round investor leverage?

**Keywords:** harsh terms, company stage, down round, investor leverage, liquidation preference, market terms.

## Dilution & Subscription Rights

### Pro Rata Subscription Rights

Do shareholders have pro rata subscription or pre-emption rights for new share issuances?

**Keywords:** pro rata, subscription right, pre-emption right, new issuance, participation right, capital increase.

### Anti-Dilution Method

Is there an anti-dilution clause, and is it full-ratchet or weighted-average, whether broad- or narrow-based?

**Keywords:** anti-dilution, full ratchet, weighted average, broad-based, narrow-based, down round.

### Anti-Dilution Sunset

Does anti-dilution protection have a sunset, such as expiry after the next round or after a defined number of years?

**Keywords:** anti-dilution sunset, expiry, next financing round, time limit, protection period.

### Pay-to-Play

Is anti-dilution protection subject to pay-to-play, so only investors participating in the down round remain protected?

**Keywords:** pay-to-play, anti-dilution, down round, participation requirement, protected investor.

### Subscription-Right Waivers

Have existing shareholders formally waived statutory subscription rights where needed for ESOP or anti-dilution top-ups?

**Keywords:** waiver, statutory subscription rights, ESOP, anti-dilution top-up, pre-emption waiver, shareholder resolution.

### Preferred Share Conversion

Is a voluntary and mandatory-upon-IPO conversion mechanism for preferred shares clearly specified, including any required approval threshold?

**Keywords:** preferred share conversion, voluntary conversion, mandatory conversion, IPO, approval threshold, ordinary shares.

## Transfer Restrictions

### General Transfer Restriction

Is there a general restriction confirming that shares cannot be transferred, pledged, or encumbered except as permitted in the SHA?

**Keywords:** transfer restriction, pledge, encumbrance, permitted transfer, disposal, shares.

### Permitted Transfers

Are Permitted Transfers, such as transfers to affiliates or ESOP-related transfers, clearly and narrowly scoped?

**Keywords:** Permitted Transfer, affiliate transfer, ESOP transfer, carve-out, transferee, transfer restriction.

### Lock-Up Period

Is there a lock-up period, and has it already expired?

**Keywords:** lock-up, transfer prohibition, lock-up expiry, restricted period, shareholder transfer.

### ROFO and ROFR Mechanism

What transfer mechanism applies—ROFO, ROFR, both, or neither—and does it create a meaningful impediment to an eventual sale?

**Keywords:** ROFO, right of first offer, ROFR, right of first refusal, share sale, transfer process.

### Drag-Along Priority

Does drag-along clearly take precedence over ROFR, ROFO, and tag-along rights in case of conflict?

**Keywords:** drag-along, ROFR, ROFO, tag-along, precedence, conflict, exit transaction.

### Drag-Along Trigger and Consideration

What threshold triggers drag-along, and must the purchase price be bona fide and paid in cash?

**Keywords:** drag-along threshold, bona fide offer, cash consideration, forced sale, majority threshold, purchase price.

### Tag-Along Change of Control

Does tag-along cover a genuine change-of-control trigger and obligate the buyer to extend the same terms to minority holders?

**Keywords:** tag-along, change of control, minority shareholder, same terms, buyer obligation, co-sale right.

### ROFR Exit Deterrence

Could the ROFR provisions realistically deter a future acquirer, and is there an expiration or drag-along override addressing this?

**Keywords:** ROFR, acquirer deterrence, expiration, drag-along override, exit, transaction delay.

### Share Pledge Prohibition

Are shares expressly prohibited from being pledged as collateral, with any carve-out narrowly scoped?

**Keywords:** share pledge, collateral, security interest, prohibition, carve-out, fund transfer.

## Leaver, Founder, Option Pool & Exit Provisions

### Founder Vesting

Is there a founder vesting schedule with a cliff and vesting period, and does it cover shares already held since incorporation?

**Keywords:** founder vesting, cliff, vesting period, reverse vesting, incorporation shares, unvested shares.

### Purchase Option Events

Is there a Purchase Option covering triggering events such as death, incapacity, insolvency, criminal acts, and good- or bad-leaver events?

**Keywords:** Purchase Option, death, incapacity, insolvency, criminal act, good leaver, bad leaver.

### Manufactured Bad Leaver

Can investors or managers manufacture a Bad Leaver event?

**Keywords:** Bad Leaver, manufactured trigger, termination, investor control, manager discretion, leaver classification.

### Leaver Pricing

Are good-leaver and bad-leaver pricing terms clearly defined, such as fair market value versus nominal value?

**Keywords:** good leaver, bad leaver, fair market value, nominal value, purchase price, leaver shares.

### Investor Put Option

Is there an Investor Put Option, including an exit at a symbolic value such as CHF 1, and is it clear when it may be exercised?

**Keywords:** Investor Put Option, put right, CHF 1, symbolic value, exercise trigger, investor exit.

### Vested Stock Clawback

Is there a clawback allowing the company to reclaim vested stock to replenish the option pool, with clearly bounded rather than open-ended conditions?

**Keywords:** clawback, vested stock, option pool, repurchase, bounded conditions, founder shares.

### IPO and Exit Restrictions

Are exit and IPO provisions addressed, including market stand-off or lock-up obligations and post-IPO registration rights?

**Keywords:** IPO, exit, market stand-off, lock-up, registration rights, public offering.

### Qualified Exit Threshold

Is there a defined Preferred Exit or Qualified Exit threshold determining when preference and conversion mechanics are triggered?

**Keywords:** Preferred Exit, Qualified Exit, exit threshold, liquidation preference, conversion trigger, transaction value.
