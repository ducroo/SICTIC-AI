# 5 SHA and Articles Executability

## Capital for the conversion

### Conditional capital or capital band in the articles

Do the articles of association provide conditional capital or a capital band whose purpose covers the conversion of convertible loans, and is the available amount large enough for the conversion of this loan together with the loans already outstanding at the cap price? Compare the capital source the term sheet relies on with what the articles actually contain.

**Keywords:** conditional capital, capital band, Kapitalband, bedingtes Kapital, articles of association, purpose, available amount.

### Shareholder resolutions and consents

If the conversion depends on a shareholder resolution or on consents under the shareholders' agreement, are those resolutions or consents already documented (minutes, written consent, signed waiver), and which shareholders could block them? Check the quorum and majority that the articles and the SHA require for a capital increase.

**Keywords:** shareholder resolution, general meeting, consent, quorum, majority, blocking, written consent, minutes.

### Pre-emption rights waiver

Do the articles or the shareholders' agreement grant pre-emption rights on new shares, and does the term sheet's provision on their reduction match a waiver that the existing shareholders have given or must give before the conversion? An unwaived pre-emption right can delay or reduce the conversion.

**Keywords:** pre-emption right, Bezugsrecht, subscription right, waiver, reduction of pre-emption rights, advance subscription right.

## My position after conversion

### Rights of the conversion share class under the SHA

Do the shareholders' agreement and the articles define the class of shares the lender receives on conversion, with its liquidation preference, anti-dilution protection, voting and information rights? Check that the class named in the term sheet exists or will be created, and how it ranks against existing preferred classes.

**Keywords:** share class, liquidation preference, anti-dilution, preferred shares, voting rights, class rights, ranking.

### Accession obligations

Which shareholders' agreement must the lender accede to on conversion, and what does accession commit the lender to: drag-along, tag-along, transfer restrictions, leaver provisions, non-compete, further financing obligations? Check whether the SHA to accede to is the current one or one to be negotiated in the round.

**Keywords:** accession, deed of adherence, drag-along, tag-along, transfer restriction, lock-up, further financing, joinder.

### Existing restrictions on new loans and share issues

Does the current shareholders' agreement or do the articles restrict the company from incurring convertible debt or issuing shares without the consent of certain investors or the board, and has that consent been obtained for this loan? A loan taken in breach of an existing SHA can be challenged by the existing investors.

**Keywords:** important matters, reserved matters, investor consent, board approval, incurring debt, issue of shares, existing investors.

## Register and consistency

### Share register and commercial register consistency

Does the share register agree with the commercial register extract and the articles on the share capital, the classes and the number of shares, and are earlier conversions recorded? The conversion arithmetic in the term sheet depends on a share count that the registers must confirm.

**Keywords:** share register, commercial register extract, share capital, number of shares, earlier conversions, consistency, register.
