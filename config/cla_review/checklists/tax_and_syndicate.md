# 4 Tax and Syndicate

## Swiss tax aspects for the lender

### Interest within the safe harbor

Is the interest rate at or below the Swiss Federal Tax Administration safe-harbor rate for the currency and year of the loan, or does the term sheet reference the safe harbor as a cap? Interest above the safe harbor risks being treated as a hidden profit distribution with withholding tax consequences; interest-free loans have their own consequences for a lender who is also a shareholder.

**Keywords:** safe harbor, ESTV, SFTA, safe-harbour rate, withholding tax, hidden profit distribution, interest-free.

### Discount and reclassification as a non-classic convertible

Does the combination of discount and interest stay within the tax administration's practice for classic convertible loans (since 1 May 2025 a total advantage above one third is treated as a non-classic convertible, with income tax on the discount for the lender)? Check whether a rising discount schedule can cross that line late in the term.

**Keywords:** discount, non-classic convertible, reclassification, one third, 33 1/3 percent, income tax, tax practice, ESTV circular.

### Stamp duty on the conversion

Does the term sheet say who bears the Swiss issuance stamp duty of 1 percent on paid-in capital above the CHF 1 million franchise when the loan converts, and whether the conversion is structured to use the franchise? Silence usually means the company pays, which reduces the money available after the round.

**Keywords:** stamp duty, issuance stamp duty, Emissionsabgabe, 1 percent, CHF 1 million franchise, conversion cost.

### Withholding tax and the 10/20 non-bank rules

Does the term sheet limit the number of lenders or the number of lenders on identical terms, so that the company stays below the 10 and 20 non-bank creditor thresholds beyond which the interest becomes subject to Swiss withholding tax? Check whether the aggregate amount and the expected syndicate size are consistent with those limits.

**Keywords:** 10/20 rule, non-bank creditors, 10 non-bank lenders, 20 non-bank lenders, withholding tax, identical terms, bond classification.

## Syndicate structure

### Pooling vehicle and syndicate agreement

Are the syndicate members expected to lend directly or through a pooling vehicle or a syndicate agreement, and who represents them towards the company? Check whether the vehicle's costs, decision rules and exit terms are described, and whether direct lenders would count separately for the non-bank rules.

**Keywords:** pooling vehicle, syndicate agreement, investment vehicle, representative, pooled investment, syndicate member.

### Accession of further lenders on identical terms

May further investors join the loan after signing, until when, and only on identical terms? Accession on better terms without a most-favoured-nation clause dilutes the early lenders; accession without a deadline keeps the aggregate amount open.

**Keywords:** accession, further investors, identical terms, accession deadline, additional lenders, joining the loan.

### Minimum ticket and allocation

Does the term sheet state a minimum ticket per lender, how allocations are made if the loan is oversubscribed, and whether the lead investor's allocation is protected? Compare the aggregate range with the expected number of syndicate members.

**Keywords:** minimum ticket, minimum investment, allocation, oversubscription, aggregate amount, cut-back.

### Execution evidence and loan register

Does the term sheet require the company to keep a register of the loans and lenders, to deliver executed loan agreements to each lender, and to record the conversion in the share register? The lender's proof of claim and later of shareholding depends on those records.

**Keywords:** loan register, executed agreement, signed copy, share register, record of lenders, evidence of loan.
