# 2 Governance and Process

## Decisions among the lenders

### Investor majority definition

How is the lender or investor majority defined (by principal, by head count, or with the lead investor's consent), and which decisions can it take for every lender? As a syndicate member with a smaller ticket, check whether a majority can waive a conversion event, extend the maturity or amend the economics without your consent.

**Keywords:** investor majority, lender majority, majority of principal, majority decision, waiver, amendment, binding on all lenders.

### Lead investor role and privileges

Does the lead investor hold rights the other lenders do not, such as a board seat, an observer right, a fee, priority information or a veto? From the member's side, check whether those privileges come at the syndicate's expense or whether the lead acts for all lenders.

**Keywords:** lead investor, board seat, observer, arrangement fee, lead rights, representative, syndicate lead.

### Amendments and waivers

Can the loan terms be amended or a right waived by the company together with a lender majority, and are the cap, the discount, the maturity date and the subordination protected from amendment without each lender's consent? Compare with the amendment clause of the SECA reference.

**Keywords:** amendment, waiver, unanimous consent, entrenched terms, consent of each lender, variation.

## Information and participation

### Information rights

Does the term sheet give the lenders periodic financial information, notice of a financing round, a change of control or an event of default, and access to the company's documents until conversion? A lender without information rights learns of the conversion event after it has been decided.

**Keywords:** information rights, reporting, financial statements, notice of financing, notice of default, access to information.

### Pro-rata participation in the financing round

Does the lender obtain a right to participate in the qualified financing round with new money in proportion to its holding, in addition to the conversion? Check whether the right exists, whether it can be transferred within the syndicate, and whether it survives conversion.

**Keywords:** pro rata, participation right, pre-emptive right, subscription right, right to participate, follow-on.

### Most favoured nation

If the company grants better terms to a later lender, does the term sheet extend those terms to the existing lenders automatically? Check whether the clause covers all economic terms and later convertible instruments, or only the interest rate.

**Keywords:** most favoured nation, MFN, better terms, later lenders, equal treatment, subsequent convertible.

## Process and documentation

### Conditions precedent and closing mechanics

What must happen before the loans are drawn (minimum aggregate amount, shareholder resolutions, due diligence, signed accession), what happens if the minimum is not reached, and are there tranches or milestones? A lender should not fund before the conditions that protect the conversion are met.

**Keywords:** conditions precedent, closing, minimum aggregate, tranche, milestone, drawdown, funding condition.

### Binding provisions and exclusivity

Which provisions bind the parties before the loan agreement is signed (confidentiality, exclusivity, costs, governing law), until when does exclusivity run, and does exclusivity bind the lender or only the company? Check whether the binding provisions commit the lender to more than the negotiation.

**Keywords:** binding provisions, non-binding, exclusivity, confidentiality, costs, governing law, term sheet effect.

### Documentation form, counsel and costs

Does the term sheet name the documentation form (SECA short form, SECA long form or bespoke), the counsel drafting it, and who pays the legal fees? Bespoke documentation and company-paid counsel for both sides are points where the lender should expect to review the drafts.

**Keywords:** documentation, SECA template, long form, short form, counsel, legal fees, costs, drafting.
