# 3 Protection and Downside

## Ranking and repayment

### Subordination scope

Is the loan subordinated, and does the subordination cover the full loan balance including interest or the principal only? Is it subordinated only in an insolvency context (article 725b CO style) or towards all creditors at all times, and can the company issue senior debt without limit? From the lender's side, the narrower and more conditional the subordination, the better.

**Keywords:** subordination, subordinated loan, Rangrücktritt, loan balance, principal only, insolvency, senior debt, ranking.

### Repayment claim and extension

If the loan is not converted, is repayment at maturity an enforceable claim, can the company extend the maturity unilaterally or with a lender majority, and is there a prepayment right? Check whether a prepayment right lets the company repay the loan just before a round to avoid the conversion.

**Keywords:** repayment, maturity, extension, prepayment, early repayment, redemption, unilateral extension.

### Events of default and acceleration

Does the term sheet list events of default (insolvency, breach, change of control without consent) and does it give the lenders an acceleration right? Compare the list with the SECA reference; a term sheet without any default mechanics leaves the lender with the general law only.

**Keywords:** event of default, acceleration, breach, insolvency, cross default, termination right.

## The shares I receive

### Share class on conversion

Which class of shares does the lender receive on conversion: the class issued to the new investors in the qualified round, with the same preferences and rights, or common shares? Conversion into a class with fewer rights than the round investors receive is a material weakness.

**Keywords:** share class, preferred shares, common shares, same class as new investors, preference, conversion shares.

### Capital sources and consents for the conversion

Does the term sheet identify how the conversion shares will be created (conditional capital, a capital band or a shareholder resolution with waiver of pre-emption rights), and who must consent? A conversion that depends on a future shareholder resolution can be blocked by shareholders who did not sign the term sheet.

**Keywords:** conditional capital, capital band, Kapitalband, shareholder resolution, consent, pre-emption waiver, authorised capital.

### Dilution before conversion

Does the term sheet restrict share issues, option grants or further convertible loans between signing and conversion, or at least require them to be disclosed? Issues at a low price before the round reduce the lender's share without affecting the cap.

**Keywords:** dilution, interim issue, option grant, further convertible, negative covenant, ordinary course, prior to conversion.

## Representations and covenants

### Representations and warranties

Does the company give representations on its capitalisation, title to assets, litigation, taxes and the accuracy of the information provided, and are they given at signing and at drawdown? Compare the scope with the SECA reference.

**Keywords:** representations, warranties, capitalisation, cap table accuracy, litigation, taxes, disclosure.

### Covenants and use of proceeds

Does the term sheet state the use of proceeds and impose covenants (no distributions, no related-party dealings, maintenance of insurance, compliance with law) until conversion? A lender funds a plan; the covenants keep the plan in place.

**Keywords:** covenants, use of proceeds, negative covenant, distributions, related party, undertakings.
