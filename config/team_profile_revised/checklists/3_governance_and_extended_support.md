# 3 Governance and Extended Support

## Ownership, control and formal agreements

### Q119 — Founder departure and leaver arrangements

Q119: Do supplied shareholders' or founders' agreements contain documented purchase options, vesting, good/bad-leaver or other departure arrangements? Identify the relevant parties and provisions and any material uncertainty about how founder departure affects equity and incentives. Cite actual clauses where available; do not assume a clause exists or is absent because a document was not supplied. Record legal interpretation questions for review rather than claiming enforceability.

**Keywords:** shareholders agreement, purchase option, good leaver, bad leaver, vesting, founder departure, equity

### Q120 — Founders' agreement and documented commitments

Q120: Is a written, signed founders' agreement or equivalent documented arrangement available covering equity, roles, governance, exit and IP? Identify the documents, parties, signature status, scope and material uncovered topics. Distinguish an unsigned draft, a stated agreement and verified signed terms. If no agreement is supplied, report what cannot be established rather than claiming none exists.

**Keywords:** founders agreement, signed agreement, equity, roles, governance, exit, IP assignment

### Q144 — Unilateral control and safeguards

Q144: What founder voting and control rights are documented, and can one founder make material decisions unilaterally? Identify reserved matters, board rights, vetoes and any material imbalance or unresolved conflict between control and other founders' obligations. Unilateral control is not automatically a defect; explain its practical implications. Assess control separately from succession and do not require a formal succession plan.

**Keywords:** voting rights, founder control, reserved matters, board rights, unilateral decisions, veto, shareholders agreement

### R004 — Employment and conflicting obligations

R004: What founder employment or service arrangements are disclosed or supplied, and are material terms documented? Assess written or reported oral arrangements, working time, compensation, notice, IP assignment and restrictive obligations. Include other key personnel only where their arrangements create a material founder-team dependency. Separate a claimed arrangement from reviewed terms and identify unresolved conflicts with prior employers or concurrent roles. Do not assume employment status from a title.

**Keywords:** founder employment, service agreement, compensation, notice, IP assignment, noncompete, working time

### R008 — ESOP and PSOP arrangements

R008: Is an existing or planned employee or phantom-share participation scheme disclosed? Report supplied plan documents, approvals, grants, vesting, the available or remaining pool, cap-table treatment and dilution assumptions in the financing plan. Distinguish planned from approved or granted interests. If no scheme information is supplied, use Insufficient information; use Not applicable only when the evidence establishes no relevant existing or planned scheme.

**Keywords:** ESOP, PSOP, option pool, phantom shares, grants, vesting, dilution, cap table, approvals

## Board and advisors

### Q022 / Q023 / Q024 / Q129 — Relevant and active extended support

Q022, Q023, Q024, Q129: Which named board members and advisors have a documented relationship and demonstrable expertise in a material area such as industry, technology, go-to-market, sales, scaling or manufacturing? Describe the person's relevant record, actual contribution, ongoing activity and available confirmation (for example an appointment, advisory agreement, minutes, correspondence or supplied reference). Reputation or a name on a slide is not proof of active support. Use only evidence in the data room; do not look up public standing or contact the person. Identify unconfirmed relationships and the exact evidence that would resolve them.

**Keywords:** board member, advisor, appointment, advisory agreement, contribution, industry expertise, sales, scaling, manufacturing

### Q130 — Documented board activity

Q130: Where a board or equivalent governance body exists, is there evidence that it actually meets or takes relevant decisions? Identify supplied minutes, dates, attendees, resolutions or follow-up actions and any material discrepancy with claimed oversight. Missing minutes are not proof that no meeting occurred. Use Not applicable only when the documented structure makes the question inapplicable.

**Keywords:** board meeting, minutes, attendees, resolutions, oversight, governance, actions

### Q132 — Advisor prominence versus operating evidence

Q132: Does the team's presentation rely materially on advisor names or prestige to substantiate execution capability without evidence of active founder ownership, concrete advisor contributions or relevant customer/commercial progress? Explain any specific mismatch between the presentation and the supplied evidence. Do not compare advisor and customer counts, assume that every venture should already have reference customers, or flag a concern solely because advisors are prominent. Cross-reference the substantive coverage and advisor checks instead of duplicating their findings.

**Keywords:** team slide, advisors, execution capability, founder ownership, advisor contribution, customer evidence, commercial progress

## Related-party interests and conflicts

### Q015 / Q017 / Q018 — Spinoff and parent-company dependencies

Q015, Q017, Q018: Is this a spinoff or closely related venture with continuing parent or existing-company involvement? Identify the disclosed financial interests, funding, founder employment, resource dependence, IP relationships and operating obligations. Explain any material effect on founder commitment, independence or alignment and distinguish supportive involvement from conflicting control or dependence. Use Not applicable only if the evidence establishes no such relationship; otherwise preserve missing information.

**Keywords:** spinoff, parent company, founder employment, related company, financial involvement, IP ownership, resource dependency

### Q124 / Q138 — Related-party transactions and interests

Q124, Q138: Do supplied ownership disclosures, contracts or other evidence show that founders hold personal financial interests in material related assets or share ownership with named customers, suppliers or contractors? Identify the parties, interest, transaction, available disclosure and practical conflict. Do not label an interest undisclosed unless the evidence supports that distinction. Missing ownership information is uncertainty, not proof of a concealed relationship or absence of conflicts.

**Keywords:** related party, beneficial owner, customer, supplier, contractor, founder financial interest, conflict of interest, disclosure
