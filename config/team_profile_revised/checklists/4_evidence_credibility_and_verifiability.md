# 4 Evidence Credibility and Verifiability

## Internal and cross-source consistency

### Q019 / Q020 — Consistency of roles and dates

Q019, Q020: Do founder roles, titles, employment and relevant dates agree across supplied applications, decks, CVs, contracts and other data-room documents? Identify specific material discrepancies and distinguish chronology or a later update from a contradiction. A LinkedIn or website statement is usable only if it is already supplied as documentary evidence. Do not search public sources. Do not interpret an outdated source as deception without further support.

**Keywords:** founder roles, titles, dates, CV, application, pitch deck, employment history, inconsistencies

### Q118 / Q122 — Consistency of statements and recorded management changes

Q118, Q122: Where statements from multiple team members or supplied commercial-register records are available, do they consistently describe management, board membership, changes and material team facts? Attribute each statement or record and its date, describe any discrepancy and whether chronology resolves it. Use only supplied records; do not claim a registry search was performed. If the relevant comparison source is absent, identify the missing evidence without inferring inconsistency.

**Keywords:** founder statements, management changes, board changes, commercial register, transcript, consistency, dates

## Independent corroboration

### Q123 / Q125 / Q127 / Q133 — Supplied diligence and reference evidence

Q123, Q125, Q127, Q133: Is prior-investor due diligence or documented reference evidence supplied that corroborates material founder claims? Identify the source, date, subject, relationship, independence and actual claim supported, including evidence from former employees, advisors, investors or customers where present. Distinguish founder-selected endorsements from independently sourced evidence; do not assume independence. If a supplied reference discusses likely reasons for early departure, record the substantive evidence without requiring that exact interview question. The agent cannot establish whether outreach was attempted or a relationship is independently reachable unless documented. Record material missing references, provenance or questions for human follow-up, without undertaking outreach or claiming contacts occurred. Do not impose an invented minimum number of references.

**Keywords:** previous investor due diligence, reference report, former employee, independent reference, customer reference, advisor reference, corroboration

## Identity, public standing and adverse information

### Q134 / R005 — Identity and operating-location evidence

Q134, R005: What supplied evidence supports founders' identity, operating location and any claimed meeting with a reviewer? Compare stated identities and addresses with provided records or documented verification, if any. Distinguish consistency of identifying information, a provided identity document and an independently completed identity check. Do not claim that the agent authenticated an ID or met a founder. Avoid reproducing passport numbers or other unnecessary identification details in the report. Missing direct proof or meeting records are Insufficient information.

**Keywords:** founder identity, identification, passport, identity check, reviewer meeting, office location, operating address

### Q136 / Q139 / R006 / R007 — Supplied debt-enforcement and criminal-record evidence

Q136, Q139, R006, R007: Are debt-enforcement excerpts (Betreibungsregister/Betreibungsauszug), criminal-record extracts or relevant reports supplied for active founders? Assess debt-enforcement and criminal-record evidence separately within the answer: subject match, issuing source, date, scope and documented entries or absence of entries. Distinguish an official extract from a company statement or promise to provide one. Explain only documented relevance to integrity or the ability to operate the venture, without claiming a broader clean history than the evidence establishes. If either record is missing or stale for the proposed conclusion, identify that limitation and a specific follow-up. Do not retrieve records or infer their contents from a lack of adverse material elsewhere.

**Keywords:** Betreibungsregister, Betreibungsauszug, debt enforcement, Strafregisterauszug, criminal record, official extract, founder background

### Q137 — Adverse-information evidence already supplied

Q137: Does the data room contain a documented adverse-media or background-search report, independent-source material or a disclosed adverse matter relevant to active founders? Identify what was actually checked, the subject, sources, dates, scope, findings and unresolved identity matches where documented. Distinguish allegation, established fact, response and outcome. If no report or source material is supplied, use Insufficient information; never claim that the agent searched public media or that no adverse information exists.

**Keywords:** adverse media, background report, independent source, founder disclosure, allegation, outcome, search report
