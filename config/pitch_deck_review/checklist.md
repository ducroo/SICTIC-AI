# Pitch deck review

## Business potential

### Customer and revenue

Does the deck say who pays, and for what?

A reader should be able to name the customer and the offer. Mark Not Found when the deck never says who pays. Mark Borderline when the customer is only a category such as "enterprises" with no offer. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the paying customer and the offer are specific.

**Keywords:** customer, buyer, revenue, pricing, who pays

### Scale of the opportunity

Does the deck give a reason this can become a large business, with a market, segment, or revenue path that is more than a slogan?

Mark Not Found when size is absent. Mark Borderline when the deck says "huge market" without a segment or source. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the deck names a segment, a figure, or a path to scale.

**Keywords:** market, TAM, SAM, segment, scale, growth

## Product innovation

### Innovative component

SICTIC asks for an innovative technology, science, or IP component that enables scalable growth. Does the deck describe what is new, and why that novelty can scale?

Mark Not Found when the deck does not describe the product's novelty. Mark Borderline when it says "AI" or "platform" without saying what is new. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the novel component is concrete.

**Keywords:** technology, science, IP, patent, innovation, product

## Team potential

### Core team

Does the deck name the core team and show why those people can build this company?

Mark Not Found when no people are named. Mark Borderline when names appear without a role or relevant background. Mark Sufficient or Fine when named people have roles and a relevant background.

**Keywords:** founder, CEO, CTO, team, background

## Document quality

### Problem

Does the deck state one specific problem and who has it?

Mark Not Found when there is no problem. Mark Borderline when the problem is generic. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the problem and the person who has it are specific.

**Keywords:** problem, pain, customer problem

### Solution

Does the deck explain the product and how it addresses the stated problem?

Mark Not Found when the product is not described. Mark Borderline when the solution does not connect to the problem. Mark Sufficient or Fine when a reader can explain what the product does.

**Keywords:** solution, product, how it works

### Market

Does the deck say which market the startup enters, and why this is the moment?

Mark Not Found when the market is absent. Mark Borderline when the market is named but the timing is missing or vague. Mark Sufficient or Fine when both the market and the timing are stated.

**Keywords:** market, why now, timing, segment

### Business model

Does the deck explain how the startup makes money?

Mark Not Found when pricing or the revenue mechanism is absent. Mark Borderline when it says "SaaS" or "marketplace" without how money is charged. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the charging mechanism is clear.

**Keywords:** business model, pricing, subscription, take rate

### Traction or current evidence

Does the deck show current evidence such as users, revenue, pilots, letters of intent, or a stated pre-revenue stage?

Mark Not Found when the deck is silent on evidence and does not say the company is pre-revenue. Mark Borderline when a claim such as "strong traction" has no number or example. Mark Sufficient or Fine when a number, a named pilot, or an explicit pre-revenue statement is present.

**Keywords:** traction, revenue, users, pilot, LOI, customers

### Funding ask

Does the deck state how much the startup is raising and what the money is for?

Mark Not Found when the amount or the use is absent. Mark Borderline when only one of the two is stated. Mark Sufficient or Fine when both the amount and the use are stated.

**Keywords:** raising, round, use of funds, ask

## Investment criteria

### Swiss or Liechtenstein core

SICTIC requires the core team, including key C-level roles, to be based in Switzerland or Liechtenstein and to work on the startup as their main activity. Does the deck say where the core team works, and that this is their main activity?

Mark Critical when the deck places the core team outside Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Mark Not Found when location is absent. Mark Borderline when a Swiss address appears but the deck does not show that the core team works there as their main activity. Mark Sufficient or Fine when both are stated.

**Keywords:** Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Zurich, Lausanne, based, full-time

### Stock corporation

SICTIC requires the startup to be incorporated, or to commit to incorporate, as a stock corporation (AG) in Switzerland or Liechtenstein. Does the deck say this?

Mark Critical when the deck states a different company form or a different country of incorporation, with no commitment to a Swiss or Liechtenstein AG. Mark Not Found when incorporation is absent. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the AG, or a commitment to incorporate as one in Switzerland or Liechtenstein, is stated.

**Keywords:** AG, Aktiengesellschaft, incorporated, Switzerland, Liechtenstein

### Equity

SICTIC invests against equity in a company registered in Switzerland. Does the deck present this round as equity in a Swiss company?

Mark Critical when the deck says the round is not equity, such as a grant-only or non-equity instrument with no equity component. Mark Not Found when the instrument is absent. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the deck states an equity round in a Swiss company, including a convertible that converts into equity.

**Keywords:** equity, shares, convertible, CLA, SAFE

### Company age

SICTIC requires the startup to be no more than five years old, measured from incorporation. Does the deck state the incorporation year or age?

Mark Critical when the stated age is more than five years. Mark Not Found when the year or age is absent. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the stated age is five years or less.

**Keywords:** founded, incorporated, established, years

### Revenue within five years

SICTIC looks for a reasonable chance of at least CHF 5 million in yearly revenue within five years after the investment. Does the deck show a revenue path that can be compared with that figure?

Mark Not Found when no revenue ambition or forecast is stated. Mark Borderline when growth is claimed without a figure or a time horizon. Mark Critical when the deck's own forecast stays clearly below CHF 5 million a year with no path past it. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the deck states a path to at least CHF 5 million yearly revenue inside five years.

**Keywords:** revenue, forecast, CHF 5M, ARR, five years

### Valuation cap

SICTIC's published cap is a pre-money valuation no higher than CHF 8 million. Does the deck state a valuation, and is it within that cap?

Mark Critical when the stated pre-money valuation is above CHF 8 million. Mark Not Found when no valuation is stated. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the stated valuation is at or below CHF 8 million.

**Keywords:** valuation, pre-money, CHF 8M, 8 million

### Round size

SICTIC's published cap is a current round no higher than CHF 2 million. Does the deck state the round size, and is it within that cap?

Mark Critical when the stated current round is above CHF 2 million. Mark Not Found when the round size is absent. Mark Sufficient or Fine when the stated round is at or below CHF 2 million.

**Keywords:** round size, raising, CHF 2M, 2 million

### More than one founder

SICTIC does not back a solo-operated startup. Besides the founder, at least one other full-time, active team member must have a key role and equity. Advisors and consultants do not qualify. Does the deck show that second person?

Mark Critical when the deck shows only one founder and no other full-time equity-holding operator. Mark Not Found when the team is not named. Mark Borderline when a second person is named without a role, time commitment, or equity. Mark Sufficient or Fine when a second full-time key person with equity is identifiable.

**Keywords:** co-founder, equity, full-time, team, advisor
