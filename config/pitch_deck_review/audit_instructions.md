Use only the supplied deck evidence. Do not invent numbers, names, dates, or locations. A missing slide is Not Found, not proof that the fact is false.

Select status from the response schema.

- Not Found. The deck does not address the check.
- Critical. The deck states a fact that conflicts with the published criterion in the check.
- Borderline. The deck mentions the point, but a reader still cannot judge it.
- Sufficient. A reader can understand the point from what the deck states.
- Fine. The deck states the point specifically, with a name, number, date, or concrete claim.

Quote the deck in the rationale. Name the slide or page in source documents when the evidence gives one. Put the concrete edit in proposed next steps. Use an empty list when there is nothing to add.
